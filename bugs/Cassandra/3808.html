Discussion
- What compaction strategy are you using? 

 Compaction can be interrupted when truncating, running SSTable maintainance tasks like scrub, or manually stopping compaction. 
So that's normal. 

 You can check compaction with  nodetool compactionstats .
- I have not set the compaction strategy, so I think it is still the default, SizeTiered. 

 Perhaps the problem is not that compaction is interrupted, but that it never resumes? The logs do say 'Autocompaction is disabled' even though I specifically ran `nodetool enableautocompaction`. 

 `compactionstats` gives me good stats for a while after restarting, but eventually just says ` pending tasks: 0`. After that, even running `nodetool compact` doesn't do anything.

However, I have another copy of the keyspace which I tried altering to LeveledCompactionStrategy and it seems to still be exhibiting the same behavior (compaction eventually quits with ~400 sstables on disk). 

 EDIT: actually the LCS table seems to have a different failure profile.

3. Instead, I get a bunch of warnings about compacting large partitions, then `WARN: MajorLeveledCompactionWriter.java:63 - Many sstables involved in compaction, skipping storing ancestor information to avoid running out of memory`, then compactionstats gives "pending tasks: 0"
