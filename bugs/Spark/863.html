Title

WorkerWatcher in Standalone mode fail to come up due to invalid workerUrl

Description
As of  this commit , standalone mode appears to have lost its WorkerWatcher, because of the swapped workerUrl and appId parameters. We still put workerUrl before appId when we start standalone executors, and the Executor misinterprets the appId as the workerUrl and fails to create the WorkerWatcher. 

 Note that this does not seem to crash the Standalone executor mode, despite the failing of the WorkerWatcher during its constructor.
