- busyjay , this is current limitation of Hive. Hive parser does NOT support 2 or more levels of "dot". For example,  
  
 select  default .src.key from src;
 
  
 will fail. This has nothing to do with reserved keywords. If you are willing to work on the new feature to support 2 or more levels of dot, I would be happy to review it. Thanks.
