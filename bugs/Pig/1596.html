Discussion
- NPE was due to some PigMapReduceCounter.PigMapCounter getting empty inputs thus not creating corresponding counters.  However, JobControlCompiler(a reader) assumes all mappers to create the counters. 
Fix can be 
 
	 Adding counter of 0 at initialization of the tasks 
or 
	 In the JobControlCompiler, assume 0 for non-existence counters. 
 

 This patch takes the first approach
- Why not just call PigMapReduceCounter.PigReduceCounter.incrementCounter(0);?
