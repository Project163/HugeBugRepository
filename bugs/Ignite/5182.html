Title

Near cache entry can miss a TTL update if an NPE occurs on the primary node.

Description
In a rare case, you might observe a confusing  GridCacheEntryRemovedException "Failed to send TTL update request"  in logs while  reading a non-expired  cache value. 

  
 [ERROR][sys-#258%expiry.EntryRemovedOnReadTest2%][root] <default> Failed to send TTL update request.
org.apache.ignite.internal.processors.cache.GridCacheEntryRemovedException

	at org.apache.ignite.internal.processors.cache.distributed.dht.GridDhtCacheEntry.checkReadersLocked(GridDhtCacheEntry.java:732)
	at org.apache.ignite.internal.processors.cache.distributed.dht.GridDhtCacheEntry.checkReaders(GridDhtCacheEntry.java:708)

 It looks like there is a race between  sendTtlUpdateRequest(UUID, GridCacheTtlUpdateRequest)  and heap entry eviction. 
This situation is currently not handled correctly, resulting in a hidden NullPointerException that aborts TTL updates. 

 Due to the nature of the TTL updates in Ignite, this problem can only occur when the TTL is updated from the backup node. 

       

 Thus, in a very rare case, we may notice a lack of updates in the near-cache and/or on "non-initiator" backups

- Problem description in more details. 

 The race may happen due to fix made in   IGNITE-13867  . 
On primary node, after ttl update we broadcasting ttl requests to near entry-readers. 

 Specifically the race may happen between read/update/etc of the cache entry and trying to get entry readers when broadcasting ttl-requests. 

 Race example in  GridDhtCacheAdapter  between  updateTtl  and  sendTtlUpdateRequest  
  
 T1-updateTtl: entry = cache.entryEx(keys.get(i));
(create heap entry)

T2-sendTtlUpdateRequest: entry = ctx.dht().entryExx(key, incomingReq.topologyVersion());
(get existing heap entry)

T1-updateTtl: entry.touch()
(mark entry as obsolete - evict it from the heap)

T2-sendTtlUpdateRequest: readers = entry.readers();
(got a GridCacheEntryRemovedException because T1 marked the entry as obsolete and set the obsolete version to EVICT_VER)
