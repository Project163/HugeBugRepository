Title

Permission changing after scan

Description
Version v0.8.10, under Arch Linux, on an ext4 formatted drive.

I'm having a problem with permissions on files when using syncthing. I have a folder (about 26GB) which I want to sync. I have two computer scan the folders (but don't allow them to sync to each other yet. After they scan the folders, both computers change the permissions of the contents to disable read, write, and any other permissions. Here is a screenshot in Syncthing after it is done:

[Image: image]

Here is a log of "ls -la" of the folder in question after Syncthing is done scanning it:

drwxrwxrwx 6 ramesh users    4096 May 25 19:33 ./
drwxr-xr-x 9 ramesh users    4096 May 25 17:09 ../
d--------- 4 ramesh users    4096 May 25 19:34 Applications/
drwxrwxrwx 5 ramesh users    4096 Apr 14 17:32 Media/
d--------- 4 ramesh users    4096 May 15 20:54 Podcasts/
drwxrwxrwx 2 ramesh users    4096 May 14 17:39 .SyncArchive/
-rwxrwxrwx 1 ramesh users      20 May 15 20:54 .SyncID*
-rwxrwxrwx 1 ramesh users     296 Feb 10 22:32 .SyncIgnore*

Within the folders, more objects of the 26GB of data have had their permissions removed, to the point where only 8mb can be read by Syncthing. This has the side effect of, when Syncthing goes back to sync, it will delete the files from the other computer. 

I don't see anything importnat in the syncthing message output (when running syncthing on the command line). Only information like:
