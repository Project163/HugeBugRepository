Title

Document removal of cold_reads_to_omit in 2.2 and 3.0 NEWS.txt

Description
Shouldn’t 2.2 be backwards compatible with 2.1?

This could be fixed by applying the patch in   CASSANDRA-9203   on the 2.2 branch.
