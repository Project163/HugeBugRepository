Title

ViewFileSystem does not honor setVerifyChecksum

Description
ViewFileSystem#setVerifyChecksum  is a no-op.  It should call  setVerifyChecksum  on the mount points.
Discussion
- Had to make a few changes to prevent  FilterFileSystem  from mangling the scheme  to the underlying filesystem's scheme.  This caused  ViewFileSystem  to instantiate the underlying  FileSystem .  These bugs also blocked being able to write tests with a custom  FileSystem .
- In addition to common tests, ran tests in hdfs and mapreduce that used  LocalFileSystem ,  FilteredFileSystem ,  ChRootedFileSystem ,  ViewFileSystem ,  HarFileSystem , etc.
