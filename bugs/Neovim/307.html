Title

SignColumn extends into EndOfBuffer zone but LineNr does not

Description
This screenshot shows how the sign column (the one with the "m" mark in it) is drawn all the way down to into the "EndOfBuffer" region, but the "LineNR" area is not:


    
Discussion

    
- There are 3 special columns: fold column, sign column, and number column. The former two extend to the end of the window and only the number column goes until the end of the buffer.

For the latter it makes kind of sense. Why would you want to see further line numbers after the end of the buffer?

So, yes, IMHO the fold and sign column should only go to the end of the buffer as well. ~~And somehow I have the feeling I once sent a patch for this very thing to vim_dev... I can't really remember, but if so, it was many years ago.~~
