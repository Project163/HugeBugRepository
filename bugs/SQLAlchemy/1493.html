Title

Unable to update MutableList string member at specific index

Description
### Describe the bug

When upgrading to SQLAlchemy 2.0 from 1.4.50, I face an issue where strings members of a `sqlalchemy.ext.mutable.MutableList` cannot be updated.

This is likely due to strings being recognized as instances of Iterable and not scalar types:
