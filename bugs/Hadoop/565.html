Title

Fix or filter findbugs issues in hadoop-tools

Description
Now that the precommit build can test hadoop-tools we need to fix or filter the many findbugs warnings that are popping up in there.
Discussion
- This is the breakdown of the existing findbugs issues. 

 
	 Streaming - 7 (mostly returning internal data) 
	 distcp - 2 (threading issues) 
	 archives - 1 (casting Configuration to JobConf) 
	 Rumen - 8 (returning internal data and serialization) 
	 extras - 2 (some things should be marked final)
- This patch fixes most of the findbugs issues, but also filters out a few of them where changes looked like they may impact the overall functionality of the code.
