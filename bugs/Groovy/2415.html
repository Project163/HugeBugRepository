Title

antlib.xml is missing from jars

Description
We just tried to upgrade our Ant based build that uses Groovy to Groovy 2.2.0, and to our surprise the jar no longer contains antlib.xml file, which basically breaks all of our Ant tasks that Ant antlibs support to bring in Groovy tasks: 

   <taskdef uri="antlib:org.codehaus.groovy" 
             resource="org/codehaus/groovy/antlib.xml" 
             classpathref="groovy.path.id"/> 

 I believe this should be fixed rather urgently as it literally prevents users to upgrade to new versions of Groovy.
Discussion
- The last version to have it appears to be 2.0.5. To be completely precise I checked 2.0.8 and it is not there.
- antlib.xml is not only missing from the embeddable groovy-all.jar, but also from the groovy-ant.jar.   

 I changed the title to reflect this.
- Thanks for reporting.   I fixed it with  this commit .
