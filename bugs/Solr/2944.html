Title

TestDistributedStatsComponentCardinality OOM when ssl + clientAuth

Description
I don't have a seed to share, but when hacking SolrTestCaseJ4 to force SSL+clientAuth in all tests, TestDistributedStatsComponentCardinality will reliably fail with an OOM.
Discussion
- Distributed cardinality involves serializing a bunch of binary data structures to send them over the wire – classes like  
HLLSerializationTest already use  @Monster  on many tests because of the amount of heap required, I suspect the overhead of SSL encryption just pushes  
TestDistributedStatsComponentCardinality over the edge.
