Title

pypy3 unit tests sometimes fail due to an attribute we expect to be an `Event` being a `bool` instead

Description
We've seen this CI failure sporadically since we fixed the pypy3 CI job. It possible an attribute in a timer is being mocked/assigned incorrectly, or there's just some difference in how pypy3 implements `Timer` objects (I believe the complaint is about a `threading.Timer()` object).

- I've dug into this as part of having a look into #6635 . It turns out we're shadowing a protected attribute of `threading.Thread` in our `celery.utils.timer2.Timer` class (under both pypy3 and other cPython) which is a boolean rather than an event. It seems like we just tend to lose the race sometimes when testing under pypy3, but this is busted behaviour on our part and should be easy to fix. I'll roll it into the changeset for that PR now.
