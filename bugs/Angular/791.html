Discussion
- The reason is that we call `ChildNode#remove()` [here][1], which is not supported on IE ([according to MDN][2]).
(For reference, this was broken back in 12be31161.)

The fix is to change `anchorLink.remove()` to something like `anchorLink.parentNode.removeChild(anchorLink)`.
