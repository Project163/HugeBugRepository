Title

WebUI table column widths broken on iPhone 12 Pro Max and wider

Description
### What happened?

On more recent iPhones with larger screens, including:
- iPhone 12 Pro Max
- iPhone 13 Pro Max
- iPhone 14 Plus
- iPhone 14 Pro Max
- iPhone 15 Plus
- iPhone 15 Pro Max

the pixel width is greater than the 419px max-width threshold for triggering the narrow-screen configuration, so some WebUI elements display incorrectly, i.e. truncating long strings and wrapping rows of buttons without proper vertical spacing, as shown in this screenshot:

[Image: IMG_7110]

The pixel widths for these devices is 428 or 430 pixels.

This is seen on Firefox on iOS (connecting a Synology version of Syncthing), but should be browser-independent.

I will provide a PR.

### Syncthing version

v1.27.8

### Platform & operating system

Linux (64-bit Intel/AMD)

### Browser version

iOS Firefox v127.0
