Title

Don't rely on disabled scanning in CDI

Description
In   CXF-7097  , a scan tag was added to turn off default application scanning.  This approach doesn't work for all packaging types, and creates a bit of an issue informing consumers. 

 Would be better if the class were just written correctly to not be discovered. 

 I have a patch, will raise after the other one.
