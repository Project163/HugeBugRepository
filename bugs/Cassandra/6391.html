Title

logback-core-1.2.12.jar vulnerability: CVE-2023-6378

Description
[URL]

 
 A serialization vulnerability in logback receiver component part of logback version 1.4.11 allows an attacker to mount a Denial-Of-Service attack by sending poisoned data.
Discussion
- From  [URL] : 

 
  Fixed vulnerability of a potential denial of service attack on a centralized logback receiver when a third party controlling a remote appender connects to said receiver and could shut down or slow down logging of events.  

 We don't ship logback in a remote configuration, and even so I don't think we need to worry about third party appenders, so I propose we suppress.
