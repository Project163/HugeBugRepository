- In GitLab by @sigmavirus24 on Aug 8, 2015, 13:11

A few things:

- Not everything reported by Flake8 can be `# noqa`'d. We regret ever having added `# noqa` and we're not adding it to new checks.

- Lambdas are slow and really not idiomatic for Python

- `import operator; operator.add` is what you want
