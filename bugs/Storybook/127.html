Title

When navigating to a story page, the wrong story's viewMode is used

Description
**Describe the bug**

When a story page has multiple stories with different `viewMode` parameters, navigating to that story page by expanding it in the sidenav takes you to the first story with a `viewMode` that's different from the one specified for that story.

**To Reproduce**

1. Create a story page with two stories
2. Assign different `viewMode` parameters to each story
3. Navigate to the story page by expanding it in the sidebar

Here's a simple repro case: [URL] You can replicate in that Storybook by clicking "Button" in the sidebar. Notice that the first story on the `Button` page has `viewMode: 'story'`, yet expanding "Button" in the sidebar takes you to `/docs/example-button--primary`, as shown in this GIF:

[Image: page-defaults-to-docs-mode]

Conversely, if you give the first story `viewMode: 'docs'` and the second story `viewMode: 'story'`, expanding "Button" will take you to `/story/example-button--primary`.

If both stories have the same `viewMode`, navigating to the page will give you that same `viewMode` (as expected). So it seems that navigating to the story page will always take you to the first story *with the `viewMode` of the second story.*
