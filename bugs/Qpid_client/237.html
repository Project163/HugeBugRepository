Title

send disposition when session recovered and consumer closed [in onMessage]

Description
When a session is recovered and consumer closed inside onMessage, or consumer closed and session recovered, the related messages are not then not considered acknowledged thereafter, however the client should send disposition to signify their delivery. Relates earlier   QPIDJMS-484   but for some additional cases, e.g other ack modes in onMessage, reversed order.
