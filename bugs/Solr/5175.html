Title

LTR queries producing out of memory issues

Description
LTR queries are producing oom and looks like the issue is same as what is described on this   SOLR-5986  .  Query that has 10 or more terms, and LTR trying to compute features for such queries lead to full heap usage, invoking full GC and stop the world.
Discussion
