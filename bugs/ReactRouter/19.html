Title

Animating with using previous pathname

Description
Animation with ` ` is a little bit confusing. The goal is to animate the entering and leaving ` `s, but what you have to do is animate the ` `. Unfortunately, when a ` ` is rendered, it grabs the most current `history.location.pathname`. This means that both entering and exiting ` `es are choosing which ` ` to render based on the current location. I was able to get around that by adding a `pathname` prop to ` `, which will prefer that over grabbing the current `history.location.pathname`.

js
