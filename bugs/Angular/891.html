Discussion
- Hi @hadley8899,

thank you for reporting! I had a quick go with your reproduction repo and even after upgrading to `8.2.0-next.1` I am able to reproduce. At first glance, only the `NgxPaginationModule` module from `ngx-pagination` is processed by ngcc, none of its components are processed and therefore not prepared for Ivy. So this does look like an issue in ngcc that we need to look into. Thanks again for reporting!
- ngcc is not dealing with the suffixed names like `__decorate$1` and `__metadata$1` correctly, which causes the package to be processed only partially. With a small fix to ngcc we can get it to work :)
