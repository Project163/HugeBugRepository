Discussion
- This seems to be a problem with the `HttpClientModule`.

What is happening here is that in the root injector we are setting `XhrFactory` to `ServerXhr`. [URL]
However this is overridden in the child injector when `HttpClientModule` is used in a lazy module because of [URL]

I think the best solution for this would be to move the registration of `XhrFactory` provider [URL] to the `BrowserModule`, similar to other Browser specific providers [URL]
