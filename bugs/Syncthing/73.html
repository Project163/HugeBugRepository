Title

Synced data total wrapping signed int

Description
I'm currently syncing several gigs of music between my phone and my laptop, and noticed that the data upload total wrapped to a negative number on the phone (but not on the laptop). The download total on the laptop is correct (currently says 3.48 GiB). My guess is that one of the values is a 32 bit and the other a 64 bit int; perhaps the counter should be changed to an explicit uint64?

[Image: screenshot_2014-05-31-12-26-19]
Discussion
- Right, yes. The counter is actually an `uint64` but is serialized as an `int` towards the UI, which is still 64 bits on 64 bit architectures but of course 32 bits in 32 bit builds. Will be fixed.
