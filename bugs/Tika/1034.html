- Another important part of the specification that is ignored by HtmlEncodingDetector is  paragraph 4.2 of the encoding spec , that specifies aliases to use for encodings. HtmlEncodingDetector simply uses  Charset.forName . 

 The specification maps some labels to DIFFERENT charsets. For instance "ISO-8859-1" is mapped to the WINDOWS-1252 encoding.
