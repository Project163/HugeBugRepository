- I could narrow down the bug a little more.  The problem does not lie in `__add__` being a "magic" method.  It is instead somehow connected with the alphabetical order of the method names. This is why Mypy does not crash when I replace `__add__` with `__sub__`.

T1 = TypeVar("T1", covariant=True)
