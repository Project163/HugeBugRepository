Title

rpc_outbound_calls_alive unlimited growth under network partition for secure connections

Description
For secure connections, we've observed rare reproducible unlimited growth of outbound alive calls metric (and memory occupied by them) in case of network failures caused by a network partition.

The first symptoms users paid attention to were restart of one of tservers due to OOM and `Command is greater than read buffer, exist data` in logs. Note that this log line could indicate another OOM-related issue as well, but in this case it turned out to be due to the handling specific to secure connections.
