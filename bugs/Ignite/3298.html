Resolution: 
The main problem of the deadlock is that eviction thread doing partition destroy (topology WriteLock) holding "ClearFuture" monitor object, while another thread can try partition rent (topology WriteLock) and stucked on obtaining "ClearFuture" monitor obj. 
Partition destroy logic was moved outside of "ClearFuture" monitor obj to Evictor (in case of fair eviction) and to GridDhtLocalPartition itself (in case of fast eviction for empty partitions).
