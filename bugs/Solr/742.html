Title

Solr war maven artifact is not signed by 'ant sign-artifacts'

Description
Because  *.war  is not included in the set of patterns of artifacts to be signed in  sign-artifacts-macro  in  lucene/common-build.xml , the  solr-X.Y.Z.war  maven artifact isn't being signed by  ant sign-artifacts  when run under  solr/ .
Discussion
- Simple patch: 

  
 --- lucene/common-build.xml     (revision 1210051)
+++ lucene/common-build.xml     (working copy)
@@ -1047,6 +1047,7 @@
 
          <fileset dir= "@{artifacts.dir}" > 
            <include name= "**/*.jar" /> 
+           <include name= "**/*.war" /> 
            <include name= "**/*.zip" /> 
            <include name= "**/*.tgz" /> 
            <include name= "**/*.pom" />
