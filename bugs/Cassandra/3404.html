- It looks like the archive commitlogs were already created, so the linking failed.  The exact steps you took during upgrade might be interesting to look at, so we can reproduce the problem. Deleting the /var/lib/cassandra/archive/CommitLog*.log files that it's failing on would at least be simple workaround.
- One option here is to reconfigure your commitlog archive command to force the link with  ln -f ... , so the startup continues, relinking the same files again, if they exist. I'm not sure of the ramifications of  not  failing the startup as above - something indeed went wrong, do we really want to continue as if nothing happened?
