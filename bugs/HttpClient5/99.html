Title

Registering RequestInterceptorLast is misleading

Description
CloseableHttpAsyncClient client = HttpAsyncClients.custom()
.addRequestInterceptorLast( new  MyRequestInterceptor())
.build();
client.start(); 
  
 We were registering our request interceptor and were trying to log out some of the important headers we wish to see on our outgoing requests, like transfer-encoding.  We found out the hard way this header is set later in processing, in the H2RequestContent class that is registered in the HttpAsyncClientEventHandlerFactory and later in IOReactor.  This is super frustrating because registering something 'last' should be the last thing that is hit before reaching out to target.  Because we it is not, we do not see the header we wish to see.   We limit the headers we expose, so turning on Wire logging or something like that is not an option for us. The way the HttpAsyncClientBuilder is written, I do not have the option of registering my own interceptor in HttpAsyncClientEventHandlerFactory or even providing my own IOEventHandlerFactory.   What do you guys suggest here? The only option I see is to manually create our async client, and due to the heavy configuration of this class, I am hoping for an alternative.  FYI- I do not need to change the outgoing request in any way, this is merely a logging issue for us.
