Title

Clarify handling of hidden items in ImmutableTree

Description
While working on   OAK-2391  , it occurred to me that  ImmutableTree  seem to  do not  hide 'hidden' nodes ( :foobar ) but  does  hide 'hidden' properties. 

  anchela , could you have a look and clarify?
Discussion
- yes... that's a bug; that should in any case be consistent. if i remember correctly we had that broken in the AbstractTree as well and only fixed it later on... obviously we forgot to adjust the ImmutableTree. the question is what is the expected behavior... if we claim that it's just a tree-decorator around the NodeState, we probably don't want to hide the hidden items.
- initial patch that doesn't doesn't apply special logic for hidden items thus making ImmutableTree just a simple tree-wrapper around whatever the underlaying  NodeState  exposes. i am pretty convinced that filtering out hidden items would have negative consequences with the various validators/editors that are making use of it. 

 Note, that this means that almost all methods of  AbstractTree  need to be overridden. maybe we should consider changing the abstract tree such that it may or may not filter out the hidden items.  mduerig , what do you think?
- At  [URL] I added  AbstractTree#isHidden  to determine for an item whether is hidden. This should help in  ImmutableTree  for not having to override all methods.
- cool, that simplifies things a lot.
