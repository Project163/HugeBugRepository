Title

HA functionality that uses ZooKeeper may experience inadvertent TCP RST and miss session expiration event due to bug in client connection management

Description
ZOOKEEPER-1702   tracks a client connection management bug.  The bug can cause an unexpected TCP RST that ultimately prevents delivery of a session expiration event.  The symptoms of the bug seem to show up more frequently on Windows than on other platforms (though it's not really a Windows-specific bug).
Discussion
- The scope of this issue is to upgrade Hadoop Common's ZooKeeper version after resolution of that bug and get the failing tests to run and pass consistently on Windows.  This bug causes sporadic failures in the following tests on Windows:  TestActiveStandbyElectorRealZK ,  TestFailoverController ,  TestZKFailoverController ,  TestZKFailoverControllerStress .
- Is this specific to Windows?  TestZKFailoverControllerStress  seems to fail intermittently for me too
