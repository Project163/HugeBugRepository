Title

[Platform][Bug] Availability Zones section is missing on the edit universe page

Description
Steps to reproduce:
1. Login to Platform 2.4 or higher
2. Click on any universe from the dashboard
3. Click Actions -> Edit Universe
4. `Availability Zones` section is missing
- The root cause was unwanted triggering of a legacy `closeUniverseDialog()` action on unmounting the `RollingUpgradeForm` component at the universe details page.
That action resets `universe.universeConfigTemplate` record in redux store and AZ component won't render anything without it.
