Discussion
- My guess is that GCInspector detected long GC before StageManager initialized stages in its static initializer. 

 Possible solution is to defer  GCInspector.register()  after  StorageService.instance.initServer()  or do explicit StageManger static initialization before registering GCInspector in  CassandraDaemon#setup() . 

 I wonder why it was not logged in system.log, since we added default exception handler before registering GCInspector.
- It's possible it was just an error in dtest, and it really was in system.log.
