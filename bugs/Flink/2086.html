Discussion
- The problem has most likely to do with serializing a  HashMap  ( ParameterTool ) concurrently. I couldn't reproduce the problem locally, thought. 

 An easy fix should be to copy the  ParameterTool  map before assigning it to the  ExecutionConfig .
- I actually could reproduce the problem. The problem is that calls to  ParameterTool#get  have side effects and change the underlying  HashMap . This is of course not thread safe.
