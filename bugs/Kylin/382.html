- Design of "Optimized the execution time of deletion tasks". 

     

 Every value needs to be updated once, and each update takes three or four seconds. When many partitions are checked at the same time, the cumulative time is very long. So, we need collect all the partitions to be deleted, reorg them to a large partition, and just update the delta log once.
