Title

Devices Announcing LAN addresses even with AnnounceLANAddresses set to off/false

Description
Ever since the 1.10 update all of my devices are announcing their LAN addresses, even after I switch the config to turn that feature off. Despite this, devices are remembering or still discovering the LAN addresses of other devices.

I was expecting that turning off the LAN announcing would return the devices to pre-1.10 operations, but that is not the case. I do not want devices announcing their LAN addresses to either the Global Discovery servers or to each other if they are on completely separate networks.
