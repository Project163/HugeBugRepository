Title

[DocDB] XClusterTestTransactionalOnly.TransactionsWithoutApply fails in TSAN

Description
Jira Link: [Link: DB-6015]
### Description

The following deadlock is causing the test to fail.

The failure is caused by inversion in the call hierarchy and data access. The desired order is:

- The xClusterConsumer creates the xClusterPoller 
- xClusterPoller creates the xClusterOutputClient
- xClusterOutputClient accesses local objects and synchronizes access to them through local mutexes

Currently xClusterOutputClient calls back into the xClusterConsumer to obtain the producerconsumermap which is protected by a mutex and that results in a potential deadlock
