Discussion
- Applied fixes in Rev. 734634 and 734635: The service reference is now to be retrieved using a getServiceReference() method which guards against the case where the service registration is already null. In addition the service registration field is cleared before the service is unregistered to prevent an IllegalStateExcetion in the getServiceReference() method in extreme cases, where the field is not null but the service has already been unregistered.
- Confirmed to work also by Clément Escoffier.
