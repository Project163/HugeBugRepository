Just another thing, for session handling I would advise using one Memcached and/or enabling stickiness from your loadbalancer to your backend. In case a users cached session object is stored on .183 and your app switches to .182, then you have a user without his session. That is if you don't have an awesome Memcached replicator amongst your daemons, which I doubt you or any of us does :)
