Title

Generic JMS connection pool should be able to renew connections after JMSException

Description
The generic jms pool does not have a mechanism to detect connection failure and dispose those connections. Adding a jms exception listener that will do that will do the trick.
Discussion
- Fixed with  [URL]
- There's a new  reconnectOnException  property on the pooled connection factory (true by default). It will cause a new ExceptionListener to be registered on underlying connection and dispose the connection from the pool when problem is detected.
