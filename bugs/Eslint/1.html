Title

The "no-bitwise" rule doesn't account for shift operators

Description
The `no-bitwise` rule warns when it encounters the use of one of the three binary bitwise operators, `^`, `|` or `&`, but it does not warn when it encounters the use of a bitwise shift operator, ` >` or `>>>`.

Both JSLint and JSHint warn on use of the shift operators when their respective `bitwise` options are set to `true`.

@nzakas was this intentional or an oversight?
