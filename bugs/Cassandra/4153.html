Title

Order and filter cipher suites correctly

Description
As pointed out in  [URL] SSLFactory.filterCipherSuites() doesn't respect the ordering of desired ciphers in cassandra.yaml. 

 Also the fix that occurred for  [URL] is incomplete and needs to be applied to all locations where we create an SSLSocket so that JCE is not required out of the box or with additional configuration.
Discussion
- Do you have an opinion on not filtering the available cipher suites at all? Why fix the ordering when we can just get rid of the filtering in first place?
- You need the filtering to ensure that you don't attempt to use an unsupported cipher suite.  We attempt to use (by default) TLS_RSA_WITH_AES_256_CBC_SHA, which fails on systems that don't have the JCE Unlimited Strength Jurisdiction Policy.  However I don't want to remove the unsupported suites from the default because most people who have JCE will actually want to use the stronger ones (and I generally like the idea of it having that functionality by default).
