Title

CreateTable should take into account blacklisted nodes

Description
During normal operations, the load balancer (LB) is responsible for moving tablets around to satisfy client requirements, including RF, placement and also the blacklisting mechanism (ensuring nodes are drained before they can be decommissioned).

We recently discovered that we can get into a state (through moving or removing nodes), where we create a table and the master assigns tablets to a TS that is blacklisted or even dead, but <60s since death. If the TS is legitimately dead, the tablet creations will fail and ultimately timeout, leading to (hopefully) a new TS being selected for that replica.

Under normal circumstances, this would not be a problem, as, given time, the operations should succeed. However, if the driver of the operations is something like cqlsh, with some predefined timeout, it could easily hit that timeout and fail the client-visible operation.

At the minimum, we should ensure the CreateTable operation takes into account blacklisted nodes and tries to not assign tablets to a TS we know is meant to go down.

As a bigger stretch goal, we should try to unify table/tablet creation and tablet reassignment, through the LB, into one location, most simply, in the LB itself, to ensure we future proof the two codepaths.
