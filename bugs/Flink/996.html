 I think the problem is multiple things: 
 
	 The registry is created multiple times per TaskManager (each time it associates with a JobManager). The JMX registration happens multiple times as well. 
	 Reporters are not shut down properly 
	 Multiple JMX reporters cannot coexist, because there is one JMX registry per JVM, and reporters would re-register the same beans 
	 When the reporter is JMX, Flink does not need to register the JVM wide JMX-based metrics. They are already there.
