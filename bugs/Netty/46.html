Title

WebSocketClientHandshaker08 and WebSocketClientHandshaker13 notify handshake future before the encoder is present

Description
We have a race in WebSocketClientHandshaker08 and WebSocketClientHandshaker13 that lead to the problem that the ChannelFuture is notified before the actual encoder was added to the pipeline.

So if the user wait on the future and start writing after it unblocks it may come to the problem that the encoder is not yet present.
