Title

StreamHandler: closes resources it did not open

Description
StreamHander::close() is called when the StreamHandler is destroyed. It fclose()s the stream that StreamHandler is writing to.

However, StreamHandler doesn't actually own that stream - the stream is passed into StreamHandler by the caller. This causes problems for apps that rely on the stream to continue to exist after the StreamHandler has gone out of scope.

A good way to reproduce this is to create a StreamHandler(STDOUT) from inside a PHPUnit test. StreamHandler::close() will close STDOUT, and then PHPUnit cannot write any more output to the console.
Discussion
