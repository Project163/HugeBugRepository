Title

VCS info is not embedded

Description
### What happened?

I tried to build a binary with goreleaser that included vcs information introduced in Go 1.18. I tried with and without passing `-buildvcs=true` to `builds.flags` to the same effect. I came across #3143 which made me think I was configuring goreleaser incorrectly. However, I inspected the linux amd64 binary of goreleaser v1.12.3 and saw that vcs infomation wasn't embedded there either:
