Title

JDBC persistence adapter, destination mbeans not visible on restart till producers or consumers reattach

Description
With the jdbc store, if there are pending messages on a restart, the destinations do not appear in jmx till they are reused. So inactive destinations are not recreated on restart. 
Any use of the destination will get the mbean to be created, new producer/consumer etc.
