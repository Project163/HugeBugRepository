### Describe the Bug

The confirmOffset may be set to -1 due to the new connection.

[Image: image]

If a connection is inited, but has yet been TRANSFER state, then the slaveAckOffset will be -1.
It will make the newConfirmOffset be a wrong value, which is dangerous.

### Steps to Reproduce

add a log, then you will see the confirmOffset may be -1 value.

### What Did You Expect to See?

Compute the confirmOffset without those new connections.

### What Did You See Instead?

The confirmOffset was set to -1.
