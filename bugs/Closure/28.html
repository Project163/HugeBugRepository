Title

incorrect extern annotation for Option

Description
What steps will reproduce the problem? 
1. var opt = new Option('hello', 1); // or some other nr of params != 4
2. compile

What is the expected output? 
No warning. All parameters in the constructor are optional.

What do you see instead?
"WARNING - Function Option: called with 2 argument(s). Function requires at 
least 4 argument(s) and no more than 4 argument(s)."

 What version of the product are you using? On what operating system? 
Windows Vista. Closure 20100330 release
