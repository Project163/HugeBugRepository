Title

"Latest" feed occasionally shows posts out of order

Description
**Describe the bug**

Occasionally, when viewing the `/latest` feed, users can see more recently published posts appearing below older posts. For example:

[Image: screenshot of DEV latest feed. A post is circled that shows it was published "1 hour ago", and above and below it are posts from 2 hours ago]

Initially I thought this was an issue coming from the API, but I think it might actually be to do with [Link: how the frontend constructs and inserts paged-in articles in the feed]. In the example screengrab above, the network responses shows that the "Tracking responses..." article should have appeared in a different part of the sequence (the surrounding articles pictured above were not returned from the same network fetch).

TL;DR - it needs more investigation, but I think the source of the issue may be in how the paging behaviour plays with the chronological feed.


**To Reproduce**

- Go to the `/latest` feed
- Start scrolling and keep an eye on the published at date on the feed cards
- Eventually you'll see some that appear out of chronological order

**Expected behavior**

The articles in the "latest" feed should appear in the order they were published at, with the newest first.
Discussion
- It looks like a way to replicate this would be
- load /latest - do not scroll down
- wait for new articles to be published (20-30 minutes is probably enough for DEV.to, basically wait for new articles to have been published) - in test you can create articles to suit your needs.
- scroll down until a request to /search/feed_content?... is made (which returns a json list of articles)
- observe the placement of the new items in the existing list (you can view the search response and text search by title on the page) 

I'm definitely able to replicate this behavior (new items appear out of order in the latest feed when scrolling). 

I think there are a few strategies that could be followed, I'm not sure any of them are obviously correct without drawbacks

- backfilling articles items to their appropriate location in the list when they're loaded from the paged request (if an article is earlier than the displayed region - it's inserted in the page you've already scrolled past) - this could cause new articles to be placed out of view (it's unlikely someone would scroll backward looking for changes often). 
- sending a "earlier than" mark based on the last article (oldest we already have) rather than a page number - this would prevent newer items (published since the page was loaded) from appearing at all, but should be easier to keep items in order. This is a change to limit what articles we request/retrieve to avoid the issue.
