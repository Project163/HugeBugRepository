Title

Wrong Generic for setKeyGroupingComparator/setKeyOrderComparator in MultipleInputsMapReduceDriverBase

Description

 Its probably just a c&p oversight since the same methods are present in the MapReduceDriverBase class. Here the generic  K2  is the mapper output key though.
