Title

camel:route-stop does not clean up resources

Description
camel:route-stop does not work properly when there are messages in flight.  
Running a route that consumes from JMS where the route hung waiting for an ack, the route will eventually be stopped. However the JMS consumer does not unregister from the broker and the brokers JMX stats still show a consumer registered.
