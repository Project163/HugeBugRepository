Title

Duplicate values in rpcDetailedMetrics

Description
In RpcDetailedMetrics init is called two times . Once for deferredRpcrates and other one rates metrics which causes duplicate values in RM and NM metrics.

- Hi  BilwaST , thanks for reporting this. I took a quick look and as far as I can tell, this was introduced by   HADOOP-11552   which initially introduced the concept of the deferred metrics. I actually disagree with you that the problem is calling  init  twice; that method simply initializes the local data structures within  RpcDetailedMetricsWithAggregation . I believe the problem is that both  rates  and  deferredRpcRates  use the exact same method names, thus the metric names overlap. We probably need to adjust  addDeferredProcessingTime  to add a prefix or suffix to all of the metric names.
