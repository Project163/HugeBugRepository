Description
When you make a v1+ release you will find your version parts function in `internal/upgrade/upgrade_common.go` will use the zero value for the Major part.

[URL]

Here you are ignoring the error produced by `strconv.Atoi` and it fails silently when it gets a string with characters in it. Due to your major version part including a `v` (`v0.10.5`) you will find `v1.0.0` gets treated as `0.0.0`. I am not sure how you would like to resolve the issue. I would just strip the `v` letter off. But I thought I would highlight it, before it becomes a real issue :)

Cheers!
