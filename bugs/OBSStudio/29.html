### Expected Behavior

Have the auto configuration wizard not Crash  OBS when a YouTube account is linked.

### Current Behavior

OBS will crash when opening the auto configuration wizard with a linked YouTube account.

### Steps to Reproduce

1. Link a YouTube account through the stream settings.
2. Try to open the auto configuration wizard from the tools menu
3. OBS will crash


### Anything else we should know?

Unlinking the YouTube account stops the crashing.
Also doesn't happen with a twitch account for example.

Doesn't happen always but often enough that I would call it reproducible on my system.
