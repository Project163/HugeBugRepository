Title

Bug in SSAO Shader on Intel graphics 3000

Description
Hey all! First off Three.js is awesome and thank you for all of your hard work on it. I've always wanted to learn more realtime 3d code and it's much easier to learn with WebGL and Three.js!

I've found an issue with the SSAO shader on the Intel integrated graphics 3000 card. I have a 13 inch Macbook pro where the intel card is the old graphics hardware it has. On another 15 inch macbook pro it switches between the intel integrated graphics 3000 and a AMD Radeon HD 6490M 256 MB. On the AMD hardware the SSAO shader works fine. On the 13 inch mac the shader shows the bug. On the 15 inch I can use a program called gfxCardStatus to force it to only use the intel graphics, and it exhibits the same issue. 

I understand this could be a driver issue, or a hardware issue, and it could be difficult to reproduce and fix. If you would like to see my code let me know and I'll send it over. But, I just wanted to let everyone know and ask if their is any way I could detect the Intel graphics and avoid adding the SSAO Shader to the EffectComposer to avoid the bug.

Image of the bug. Top image is on Intel Integrated Graphics 3000, lower is working correctly on the AMD graphics.
