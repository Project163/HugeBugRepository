Title

facet.heatmap doesn't honor fq tag exclusion in distributed search

Description
Excluding a tagged filter query on a heatmap facet doesn't work in distributed (sharded) mode – it's effectively ignored.  Aside from being a bug in semantics (more counts should be returned), it can also thwart an optimization of   SOLR-10499  .
