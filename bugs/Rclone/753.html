- I managed to replicate this.

It is to do with the `--progress` flag you are setting in the environment.

For some reason this locks up just as you say under powershell.

    ./rclone.exe lsd -P .

It also locks up under cmd and on linux so I suspect you didn't have the `RCLONE_PROGRESS` env var set in your cmd test.

Try this and see if it fixes it
