Title

Pig distribution for hadoop 2

Description
Currently Pig distribution only bundle pig.jar for Hadoop 1. For Hadoop 2 users they need to compile again using -Dhadoopversion=23 flag. That is a quite confusing process. We need to make Pig work with Hadoop 2 out of box. I am thinking two approaches: 

 1. Bundle both pig-h1.jar and pig-h2.jar in distribution, and bin/pig will chose the right pig.jar to run 

 2. Make two Pig distributions for Hadoop 1 and Hadoop  

 Any opinion?
Discussion
- We should go with 1. It is easy to do and one installation works for both Hadoop 1 and 2 depending upon what HADOOP_HOME or HADOOP_PREFIX points to. We just check for presence of hadoop-core*.jar in hadoop classpath and if present put pig-h1.jar in classpath else put pig-h2.jar in classpath.
- +1 for 1.  IIRC bin/hadoop has a -version option, so we don't even need to depend on magic jars being present, we can just ask hadoop.
