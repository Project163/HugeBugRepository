Title

Soap Fault fields precedence is incorrect

Description
According to JSR224 spec chapter  , the the fields of the fault message are populated according to the following rules of precedence: 
 
	 faultcode 
	 faultstring 
	 faultactor (role in SOAP 1.2) 
	 detail 
 

 But in current impl of cxf 2.6.2, detail is before faultactor(role in SOAP 1.2).
