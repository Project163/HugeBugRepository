Make DISTINCT(*) a synonym for DISTINCT *

Description

It turns out that there is a substantial set of users abusing thsi property do do DISTINCT  to mean DISTINCT *. 
Instead of rolling back the entire improvement, let's special case DISTINCT ( *)
