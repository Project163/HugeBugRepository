Discussion
- Thanks for the report!

I can reproduce this with a modified version of the i386 Dockerfile used the last time a 32bit issue showed up.

Crafted what I think is the right fix now (namely, not using `sys.maxsize` under 64bit systems as a lazy shorthand for "something that rounds to 0 when you add 1 and then bitwise AND with a 32-bit full integer").
- Thank you for the fix!
