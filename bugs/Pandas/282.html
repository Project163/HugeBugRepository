Title

Timestamps don't always get the right tzinfo attached

Description
Each timezone in pytz has a `_tzinfos` dict:

In [3]: type(pytz.timezone('US/Eastern'))
Out[3]: pytz.tzfile.US/Eastern

In [4]: pytz.timezone('US/Eastern')._tzinfos
Out[4]: 
{(datetime.timedelta(-1, 68400),
  datetime.timedelta(0),
  'EST'):  ,
 (datetime.timedelta(-1, 72000),
  datetime.timedelta(0, 3600),
  'EDT'):  ,
 (datetime.timedelta(-1, 72000),
  datetime.timedelta(0, 3600),
  'EPT'):  ,
 (datetime.timedelta(-1, 72000),
  datetime.timedelta(0, 3600),
  'EWT'):  }

Need some kind of sane (read: performant) way to retrieve the right one of these to attach when boxing a timestamp. Is not as trivial as I thought
