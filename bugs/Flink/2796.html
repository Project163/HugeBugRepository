Title

Fink Mesos allocates extra port when not configured to do so

Description
Internal testing has revealed a small bug in the way LaunchableMesosWorker handles the absense of the new  mesos.resourcemanager.tasks.port-assignments  config option. 
It allocates an extra mesos port even when the option is not set.
