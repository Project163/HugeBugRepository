Title

Clicking on player covers all other content on screen

Description

- Thanks @ojw28 . My use case is similar to the demo project, with a few significant differences ... My containing Fragment view is a `MotionLayout`, and in its initial state the player isn't displayed at all (it's `gone`).  Then, upon some event (user clicks a button), we load a video stream and transition the `MotionLayout` to show the player. The stream that gets loaded can vary somewhat (though most are either 16:9 or 4:3), which precludes me from setting a fixed height. And while I can calculate the height easily enough, `MotionLayout` does not give me a way (that I know of) to set transition states dynamically at runtime.

FWIW, I tried copying the `exo_styled_player_view.xml` and `exo_styled_player_control_view.xml` and making my own versions with wrap_content on the various elements to no avail.
