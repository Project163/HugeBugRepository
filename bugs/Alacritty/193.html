Title

Vi cursor should recover position after leaving search

Description
When starting the search with the vi cursor in the last line, it will automatically get pushed up to keep it in place relative to the grid's content. However when the search is cancelled without picking any match, the cursor is not moved down again.

Ideally my vi cursor position should not change at all when entering and leaving search, even if the cursor is at the far bottom. At the very least outside of the alt screen this should be possible and I believe it has worked this way in the past.

**Edit:** I have confirmed that this worked in the past (tested with v0.5.0).
