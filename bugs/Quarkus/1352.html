Discussion
- Do you have a minimal reproducer?
- Hm, I think that it might be connected to the fact that as of 2.3 we [Link: remove the _unused_ interceptors] from the app. And given the fact that interceptors bound to a rest client interface represent a non-standard way of interceptor association it's very likely that this optimization is the cause of this problem.

You can try to disable this optimization completely via `quarkus.arc.remove-unused-beans=false`. A workaround is to list the interceptor classes in the `quarkus.arc.unremovable-types` property.

The correct solution would be to detect the interceptors associated with all rest client interfaces and make them unremovable via the `UnremovableBeanBuildItem`.
