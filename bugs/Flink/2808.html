- Hi  kien_truong , Flink currently does not fully support checkpointing of cyclic topologies. See   FLINK-3257   for more information. Part of it is that the streaming iteration tasks don't honour the checkpoint lock. That's why we see the NPE because a concurrent checkpointing operation can reset the  targetBuffer  in  SpanningRecordSerializer .  

 Since this is a known limitation, I will unblock  1.6.0  from this issue.
