- I worked with  jfchen@us.ibm.com . Then, I identified that a root cause is an integer overflow in a Java expression. 

 When SIGSEGV occurred, we identified  dest  is less than 0 at  RadixSort.java . It should be greater than 0. It means that  offsets[bucket]  is less than 0. 
Next, when we checked  transformCountsToOffsets() , we knew that  outputOffset  is less than 0. 
Then, when we checked  RadixSort.java , we knew that  outindex = 0x10?????? . Since the result of multiplication is  0x80?????? , it means that less than 0 as int. This is because  outIndex  and  8  are integer type. Finally, we understood integer overflow occurs. 

 Here, the expression must be  array.getBaseOffset() + outIndex * 8L .
