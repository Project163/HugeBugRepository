Title

cursoranim test only displays a white square

Description
On X11 and wayland backends cursoranim only switches the cursor to a white square

mesa: 10.2.7  xorg: 1.16.0

[AMD/ATI] RV710/M92 [Mobility Radeon HD 4530/4570/545v]
Discussion
- @elmindreda i tried to run cursoranim on a nvidia card and i could reproduce it if i opened weston with the x11 backend
- I have just tried cursoranim with latest master on two Linux computers (both with X11, no trace of Wayland). Results:
1. CentOS 6.5 with GNOME 2.28, Nvidia GeForce GT 520, NVIDIA drivers (version 340.?). Cursor animates correctly.
2. Debian 7.6 with IceWM 1.3.7, ATI Radeon HD 3470, Mesa 8.0.5. Cursor becomes a solid white square (as reported by @ricardomv).

However...

As an experiment on computer 2, I tried changing the pixel values written by cursoranim (from transparent white #ffff to transparent red #f00f) and got very strange results. It turns out it is very difficult to accurately screenshot the cursor, so please excuse me taking a photo of the screen.

[Image: img_20140917_161556]

It seems as though the cursor is being blended with the screen pixels in a strange way, so that the colour of the destination pixel is influencing the blending factor. In particular, a black destination pixel will be overwritten irrespective of the alpha of the cursor image, hence the appearance as a solid square within the client area.
