Discussion

    
- Seems like there is an inconsistency. For statically declared components, the behavior collection loads and sets them as controller properly but doesn't do the same for those loaded on fly. That's why for the former even after unloading the controller properly remains.

Out of curiosity why are you unloading a component? You could just disable it to prevent it's callbacks from being run.
- unloading helpers and behaviors also does not unset the properties. I don't think calling unload on a collection should reach behind and modify the object owning the collection.
- For me, both scenarios do not trigger a fatal error. Given that this behavior is consistent across all the collection/registry objects I don't think changing just Components is a good idea. Nor do I think that having the collection object sneakily unset other properties is a good idea, as those properties may not exist, or may contain other data. For example:
