Title

RSS feed GUIDs are not unique enough

Description
### Description

Running Gitea 1.17.2, RSS feed entries have a GUID which is an integer, i.e.

```
 
....
 8669 
 Sat, 15 Oct 2022 13:46:05 +0300 
 
```

Article GUID is supposed to [Link: globally identify] it between any amount of other RSS feeds. An integer value is not good enough here and may easily clash with other entries. Something like a tag: URI or a simple permalink would work a lot better. 

Even removing this field entirely would be better than current behavior. Please consider fixing this, thanks!

1.17.2
