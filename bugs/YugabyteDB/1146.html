Title

[xCluster] Replication status reported as UNINITIALIZED after master leader failover

Description
Jira Link: [Link: DB-11531]
### Description

When there are lots of tablets in the universe (>5000) we fail to initialize the replication error on a master after it fails over.

This will clear out if the tserver restarts, or if there are any actual errors in the system. It just causes some log noise.
