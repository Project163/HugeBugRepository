Title

Apt parser garbles some special characters inside tables

Description
Apt snippet: 

  
   \~, \=, \-, \+, \*, \[, \], \<, \>, \{, \}, \\.

*---------------------------------------------+--------------+
| \~, \=, \-, \+, \*, \[, \], \<, \>, \{, \}, \\.     | Apt special characters
*---------------------------------------------+--------------+
| $\{JAVA_HOME\}                             | The JAVA_HOME environment value.
*---------------------------------------------+--------------+
 
  

 The characters <, >,  
{, }
 , \ are not rendered inside the table. This worked in Doxia 1.0.
