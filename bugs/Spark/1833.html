- The meaning of the error is that Java thinks two serializable classes are not mutually compatible. This is because two different serialVersioUIDs get computed for two copies of what may be the same class. If I understand you correctly, you are communicating between different JVM versions, or reading one's output from the other? I don't think it's guaranteed that the auto-generated serialVersionUID will be the same. If so, it's nothing to do with big-endian-ness per se. Does it happen entirely within the same machine / JVM?
