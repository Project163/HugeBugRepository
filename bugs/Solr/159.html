Title

DisMaxRequestHandler crashes on badly formed query strings ( combinations of "-" and "+" )

Description
The DisMaxRequestHandler parser crashes on strings which contain double dashes or various combinations of - and + like: 

 chocolate cookie - 
chocolate -+cookie 
chocolate --cookie 
chocolate - - cookie
