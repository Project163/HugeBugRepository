Title

   </configuration>

- The problem does not occur if a '_' is used insead of the '-' sign. I guess it's related to the definition in  [URL]

   category-expression.jj   
 <STRING: ([ "A" - "Z" ,  "a" - "z" ,  "0" - "9" ,  "_" ,  "$" ,  "#" ,  "." ])+ >
 
  

 which might be  

   category-expression.jj   
 <STRING: ([ "A" - "Z" ,  "a" - "z" ,  "0" - "9" ,  "_" ,  "-" ,  "$" ,  "#" ,  "." ])+ >
- This also happens when trying to include groups, and the only workaround seems to be to rename the test group.
