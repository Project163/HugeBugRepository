Title

Inconsistent Little Endian Buffer Contents in 4.0.20.Final When Unsafe Enabled

Description
I'm sorry the details on this are light, but basically everything worked in 4.0.19.Final. After updating to 20 I get inconsistent values when getting/reading from buffers. I've gone so far as to print the buffer's reader index and contents and then watching as it reads a value that isn't consistent with what was just printed.

The scary part is if I turn unsafe off (-Dio.netty.tryUnsafe=false) everything works fine again.

Hints:
Happens with pooled and unpooled buffers.
All my buffers are immediately changed ByteOrder.LITTLE_ENDIAN in handlers.
Mac OS X 10.9.3
JDK 8u5
