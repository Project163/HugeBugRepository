- This is a bug (more than one actually).

I've a question regarding the login URL, are the parameters (`username`, `password`, and `LoginFormSubmit`) really for the URL or that's for POST data?
- It's for POST data, however, as you can probably tell by the code, i'm not particularly (read not at all) strong in Python, nor the Zap Api.

What I have so far is what I've cobbled together from here there and everywhere! I was under the belief that authmethodconfigparams would get posted?

Is there a 4th parameter for set_authentication_method that takes POST data then?
- Yes, but that's another parameter in `authmethodconfigparams`, called `loginRequestData`, e.g.:
```Python
authmethodconfigparams = ''.join(("loginUrl="+target+"login/", '&loginRequestData=', urllib.quote_plus("username={%username%}&password={%password%}&LoginFormSubmit=1")))
```
