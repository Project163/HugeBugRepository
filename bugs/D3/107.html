Title

d3.geo.transverseMercator needs special clipping.

Description
The antimeridian is rotated!
Discussion
- Alternatively transverseMercator could use normal clipping, but wrap projection.rotate and projection.stream. The wrapped rotate would implicitly add a 90° rotation on gamma, but the wrapped stream would apply the reverse rotation in projected coordinates after the normal stream is done (around the projection’s center). I’m not sure which approach is simpler, although this wouldn’t require a separate d3.geo.transverseMercator.raw.
- We could always do this and then consider replacing with equatorial cutting in future (which will probably be simpler).
