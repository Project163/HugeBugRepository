**Describe the bug**

There is an issue with `column_property` marked `deferred=True` and CTE (but maybe it happens in other cases than CTE ..)
SQLAlchemy loads column in the WITH RECURSIVE clause (which shouldn't as they are `deferred=True`) but not within the SELECT clause. Using explicit `orm.undefer()` or `orm.undefer_group()` doesn't resolve the issue.
