Title

Scanner prefetching leaks scanners.

Description
Running YCSB workload-e on 0.95 basically hangs the whole cluster. 

 YCSB opens a scanner for 100 rows. 

 
	 YCSB Calls next 
	 Pre-fetching starts 
	 YCSB Closes scanner 
	 Pre-fetching re-adds the scanner.
