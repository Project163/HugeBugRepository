Title

Reconfigure doesn't work for XmlConfiguration Log4j1 compat

Description
There is a property log4j1.monitorInterval to control how much time wait between the reconfiguration. However, in the org.apache.log4j.config.Log4j1Configuration (superclass of XmlConfiguration and PropertiesConfiguration) always returns null for the method  reconfigure , causing the reconfiguration never to succeed.
