Title

The SplitFetcherManager.close() can chain the futures in a tight loop.

Description
This is an issue introduced in   FLINK-35924  . The SplitFetcherManager.close() may chain the fetcher status check to the element queue availability future in a tight loop. This may cause problems such as OOM if the fetcher closure takes long. 

 The fix is straightforward. We just need to blocking waiting on each check.
