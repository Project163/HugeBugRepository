Title

BC Break for fixture parameters.

Description
### What steps will reproduce the problem?

Execute command

`yii fixture "*" -User`
### What is the expected result?

Loads all the fixtures except for the `UserFixture`
### What do you get instead?

Tries to determine the `-User=1` alias which doesn't exist for the fixture controller.
