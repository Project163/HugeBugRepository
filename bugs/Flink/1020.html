Title

FileSourceFunction not adjusted to new IF lifecycle

Description
The InputFormat lifecycle was extended in  , adding additional open-/closeInputFormat() methods. 

 The streaming FileSourceFunction was not adjusted for this change, and thus will fail for every InputFormat that leverages these new methods.

- it also starts the function regardless of whether splitIterator.hasNext() returns true or false. This may cause issues with NonParallelInputFormats when the parallelism of the FileSourceFunction is greater than 1.
