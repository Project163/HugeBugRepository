Thanks for reporting.
- @merryzhou I've tested your case more thoroughly and here is my take:
i) if you remove the `operationId`  from the spec, everything goes smooth
ii) specifying `operationId`   makes it a duplicate entry across the board of operations, which is why it fails. The expanded spec is invalid.
iii) there is indeed a bug, but it is a spec validation bug: generation should have detected the duplicate operationId and failed before generation.
