Title

Consider extending max limit for precision to 38

Description
Max precision of 38 still fits in 128. It changes the way you do math on these numbers though. Need to see if there will be perf implications, but there's a strong case to support 38 (instead of 36) to comply with other DBs. (Oracle, SQL Server, Teradata).
Discussion
- Thought about this some more and did some testing. Comparing storing two's complement in two longs v packing 9 digits shouldn't have a performance impact. The latter method does seem make implementation easier (add/sub will not overflow, multiplication won't overflow in long). So, all in all it's better to be aligned with other DBs than gain a little more leeway in implementing these operations.
- It would be good to have this in 0.11 as well, so we don't have different max limit between versions.
