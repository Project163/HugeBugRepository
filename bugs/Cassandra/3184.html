- I need to confirm, but it seems likely that this is related to   CASSANDRA-8033  .
- I was incorrect, it was not related to 8033.  The problem is triggered by secondary index lookups on collection "keys" (set items, map keys) where a prefix of the clustering columns are specified in the WHERE clause. 

 The attached patch fixes the issue and adds a unit test that reproduces it.
