Title

Subtitle track selection resets after switching audio track

Description
@tonihei Here's another track selection issue (this one was reported to me by @DarremMolko).

I added this [Link: test5.mkv] file to the json in the ExoPlayer Demo.

Steps:

1. Switch the subtitle from the default "English" to let's say "German".
2. Switch audio from "Auto" to "English, Mono".

The subtitle selection then resets back to English. (This applies also the other way round - switching audio first and then subtitle.) This wasn't happening in 2.15.1.

- ExoPlayer version number: `dev-v2-r2.16.1` (head currently at  )
- Android version: Android 10
- Android device: OnePlus 7
