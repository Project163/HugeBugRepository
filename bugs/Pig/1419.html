Title

Bundle dependent jars in distribution in %PIG_HOME%/lib folder

Description
Pig should bundle with dependencies like avro.jar and json-simple.jar
Discussion
- Actually not just avro/json jars. We don't ship dependent jars (except jython.jar) in Pig releases, user need to find all those jars themselves. I would suggest to bundle more jars in Pig distribution. I am thinking of two ways: 
1. bundle all build dependent jars in distribution 
2. hand pick several jars in distribution (hbase*.jar, json*.jar, avro*.jar, etc) 

 #1 is apparently more maintainable, however, more jars means more chance user get a jar conflict. Thoughts?
- I am in favor of #2 to be safe (i.e. avoiding jar conflicts). 

 Can we add groovy jars to the distribution? Someone was complaining about a ClassNotFoundException while using a groovy UDF.
