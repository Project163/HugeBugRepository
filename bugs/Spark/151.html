Title

Spark UI's do not bind to localhost interface anymore

Description
When running the shell or standalone master, it no longer binds to localhost. I think this may have been caused by the security patch. We should figure out what caused it and revert to the old behavior. Maybe we want to always bind to `localhost` or just to bind to all interfaces.
