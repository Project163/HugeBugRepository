Description

Discussion
- More specifically, at least swapping to slf4j for the producer and consumer related stuff will allow users to use logging frameworks other than log4j, since it currently does not compile without that as a dependency.
- As as workaround, you can exclude log4j from kafka, and just use the org.slf4j:log4j-over-slf4j instead.  That feeds in most log4j commands to slf4j, and then logback or another slf4j binding can be used.
