Title

The validation for the presence of a slash in the computed column fails, causing the model save to fail

Description
The use of the escape character ` ` (backslash) in the computed column causes the validation to fail, whereas the expected behavior is to save successfully.
Discussion
- Root Cause & Design 

 `StringHelper#findQuoteIndexes` encounters escape characters `\` when searching for matching single-quote strings. This causes single quotes to be incorrectly identified as regular characters, leading to mismatched pairing. Add handling for escape characters `\`.
