Title

SpeculativeScheduler do not handle allocate task errors when schedule speculative tasks may causes resource leakage.

Description
When the SpeculativeScheduler allocates slots for speculative tasks, exceptions may occur, but there is currently no exception handling mechanism in place. This can lead to resource leakage (such as   FLINK-32768  ) when errors occur. In such cases, a fatalError should be triggered.
