Title

Knobs: Wrong URI-encoding in torn-away iframe

Description
**Describe the bug**
The torn-away ` ` with a knob value changed has wrong URI-encoding, not properly escaping `%` character.

**To Reproduce**
Steps to reproduce the behavior:
1. Clone [URL]
2. `yarn install`
3. `yarn storybook`
4. Open [URL]
5. Replace `aria-label` knob content with `10% 20% 30%`
6. Click on torn-away button (An icon with square with up arrow)
7. Inspect `aria-label` in ` ` with DOM inspector

BUG: `aria-label` shows `10%%2020%%2030%`.

**Expected behavior**

1. The `aria-label` shows `10% 20% 30%`.
2. The URL shows `[URL] (`aria-label` having `encodeURIComponent('10% 20% 30%')`) instead of `10%%2020%%2030%`
