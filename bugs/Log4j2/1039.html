Discussion
- After investigating, this is due to the CassandraDaemon (in the CassendraRule) calling System.exit(..) when on Windows. 
The attached patch-log4j2-1850.diff prevents System.exit(..) from completing by installing a temporary security manager that denies the vmExit permission. The previous security manager is restored after the call to daemon.stop().
