Discussion
- The call to LocalPeerAppendFinished happens as part of the appender callback. PeerMessageQueue destruction happens as part of destroying the RaftConsensus. This appears to be a test-only issue, and the fix is to follow the same order of destruction operations that is used in production (in TabletPeer):

1. TabletPeer::StartShutdown shuts down the consensus.
2. TabletPeer::CompleteShutdown closes the log.
3. TabletPeer::CompleteShutdown destroys the consensus object.
