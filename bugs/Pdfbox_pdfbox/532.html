 Solution for me:  
1) I'd delete WinAnsiEncoding.getName() 
2) I'd set Encoding.getName() and Encoding.getCode to final, in the hope that nobody ever tries to override it again 
3) I'd delete MacRomanEncoding.getName(); 
4) I'd add    
codeToName.put( 0312, "space");     
at the end of the constructor in MacRomanEncoding.java
