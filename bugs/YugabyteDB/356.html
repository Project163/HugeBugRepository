Title

[docdb] Losing raft leadership if applying write takes too long

Description
Jira Link: [Link: DB-1746]
I was looking at some more recent test failures. Have noticed stuff like this recently: [URL]

`CreateNamespace` being slow has consistently been a thing. Recently @iSignal split it so instead of 1 massive 64MB+ raft write, it at least goes in smaller batches (10k items at a time by default).

What I noticed were CatalogManager errors like `Operation submitted in term 2 cannot be replicated in term 3`. This felt strange, as it suggests the master leader lost leadership. But from the logs, it felt like it was clearly making progress, replicating batches…slowly, but steadily!
