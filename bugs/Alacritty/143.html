Title

Line rotation invalidating grid

Description
It seems like our rotation of the grid does not behave correctly. This means that it is possible to delete history outside of the visible region by repeatedly running the escape to scroll down the viewport.

The rotation also does not update the display offset, which could potentially lead to index out of bounds crashes.

This can be reproduced like this:
 * `echo "test"`
 * `ls -lah` until "test" is gone in history
 * `echo -e "\e[1000T"`
   -> Content is incorrectly deleted from history

 * `echo "test"`
 * `ls -lah` until history is present
 * `sleep 3 && echo -e "\e[10T"`
 * Scroll to top of history
   -> Content scrolls down, display offset is beyond its limit
