Title

FormatConverter::convertDateIcuToPhp() mishandles `a` symbol

Description
ICU treats the `a` symbol as an _uppercase_ AM/PM marker, as demonstrated by the example table [Link: here]:


As expected, `Yii::$app->formatter->asTime('now', 'a')` will return `AM` or `PM`.

However `yii\helpers\FormatConverter::convertDateIcuToPhp()` translates `a` ICU symbols right back into (lowercase) `a`, which in PHP represents a _lowercase_ `am` or `pm`, per [Link: PHP’s date() documentation]:


This results in inconsistent casing on AM/PM output, depending on whether the time was formatted via ICU or PHP. (See craftcms/cms#2218 for an example.)

### What steps will reproduce the problem?

Call `yii\helpers\FormatConverter::convertDateIcuToPhp('a', 'time')`.

### What is the expected result?

Either `AM` or `PM` will be returned.

### What do you get instead?

Either `am` or `pm` will be returned.
