Title

java.lang.ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException during MOV playback

Description
Playback of `Big Buck Bunny.mov` fails due to `java.lang.ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException`.

This works in the DEMO app - however due to the encoding of the movie on my end, the playback in a integrated Exo Player fails.

Version is from git:   (dev branch).

I emailed the link to the G-Drive download to dev.exoplayer@gmail.com, along with the `adb bugreport` output, right after the crash occurred.


Discussion
- Here's another mp4 parsing failure. `srcPos=-8 dst.length=8192 dstPos=0 length=8` looks suspicious ;).
- The QuickTime format has a very peculiar format for the udta atom(not present in the MP4 format). A fix/workaround will be available for this soon. Thanks for the issue!
- ... [Middle Discussions Snipped for Brevity] ...
- @dgreenhalgh, If you are 100% sure that this bug is affecting you,  @andrewlewis suggested, one quick workaround would be to remove TYPE_udta and TYPE_meta from the shouldParseContainerAtom and shouldParseLeafAtom methods in the Mp4Extractor class.

The fix will very likely be available next week in the dev branch.
- @AquilesCanta Thanks! This workaround works for us. Looking forward to the official release.
