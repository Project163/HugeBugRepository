Title

CXF 2.3.x does not configure the Future TTL value for Timestamp processing

Description
CXF 2.3.x does not configure the Future TTL value for Timestamp processing, meaning that any future dated timestamps (more than 60s in the future) are rejected, and cannot be accepted by configuration.
