17. At the end of `Curl_disconnect`, `conn_shutdown(conn)` is called. Wouldn't this be a more appropriate place to clean up `conn->hequic[i]` here (where i is 0 and 1 because it is apparently used for IPv4/IPv6 connection attempts)?
