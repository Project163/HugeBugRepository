- Aside from why elevate.xml isn't found, I think this demonstrates a different and potentially much larger problem... 

 We call  inform(this)  from the SolrCore constructor, which then gets used in a bunch of ways. I haven't traced through yet to determine if they are all explicitly safe. NetBeans calls this  LeakingThisInConstructor , I'm sure other tooling has similar names for it. There's lots of reading about why this can introduce subtle bugs. This is probably a larger refactoring than I care to undertake right now.
- Actually, figuring out why this is looking for  elevate.xml  would also be a really interesting problem, since the  _default  config set doesn't do any query elevation. Maybe the tech products config is still lingering and we failed to delete it?
