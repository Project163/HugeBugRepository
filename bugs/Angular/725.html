Title

DatePipe does not Round 99.5 to 99.9 Milliseconds correctly

## Current behavior
 
The angular date pipe rounds the milisecond portion of the format without allowing a round upward from 99 to 100 to increase the the value in the second's position, producing both the wrong value and the wrong number of digits in the formatted output.

{{3999 | date:'m:ss.SS'}}   ---->  0:03.100

reproduced here:
[URL]

## Expected behavior
 
{{3999 | date:'m:ss'}} ----> 0:03
{{3999 | date:'m:ss.S'}} ----> 0:03.9
{{3999 | date:'m:ss.SS'}} ----> 0:03.99
{{3999 | date:'m:ss.SSS'}} ----> 0:03.999
_(edit by vicb)_

## Minimal reproduction of the problem with instructions
{{3999 | date:'m:ss.SS'}}   ---->  0:03.100

reproduced here:
[URL]

## What is the motivation / use case for changing the behavior?
The date pipe does not format timestamps correctly, which is something that I'd imagine plenty of people want to use it for.
Discussion
- Although 0:03.100 is wrong, `DatePipe` should be used to display date, not time intervals and in my opinion it should not be rounded. For example, `2018-06-08T23:59:59.999` should not be rounded to `2018-06-09T00:00:00.00` in any circumstance.
- imho the expected behavior should be

{{3999 | date:'m:ss'}} ----> 0:03
{{3999 | date:'m:ss.S'}} ----> 0:03.9
{{3999 | date:'m:ss.SS'}} ----> 0:03.99
{{3999 | date:'m:ss.SSS'}} ----> 0:03.999
- I agree with both comments above.
