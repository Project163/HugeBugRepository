- Aidan, there isn't much to go on in this JIRA but, if acknowledgeThis() is supposed to only acknowledge the given message then I think you need a more robust test.

 Whilst not related to your changes I am surprised that 0-8 and 0-10 do radically different things in their acknowledgeMessage(tag,multi) methods. 

 For instance I would expect performing a multi ack to sweek the _unackedMessageTags to remove values lower than the specified tag. 

 So I would expect this test to fail: 
send(4) 
receive 1 
receive 2 
receive 3 
receive 4 

 // perform a multiple ack of message 2 directly with AMQSession 
AMQsession.acknowledgeMessage(2, true); 

 //Ack the rest of the session. 
msg4.ack or session.ack 

 The error will occur as msg 1 should have been acked twice as the session.ack just goes through the list of _unackedMessageTags and acks them all.
