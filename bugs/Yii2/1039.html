Title

Inflector::camel2words() adding extra spaces before uppercase letters

Description
As of Yii 2.0.43, `Inflector::camel2words()` is inserting extra spaces before uppercase letters that are already preceded by a space.

### What steps will reproduce the problem?

Run the following code:

```php
echo Inflector::camel2words('Foo Bar');
```

### What is the expected result?

Output:

    Foo Bar

### What do you get instead?

Output:

    Foo  Bar

(two spaces)

This was introduced by #18807 in 2.0.43. The spaces were getting added by the `preg_replace()` in `camel2words()` before that, but then they were getting removed by `StringHelper::mb_ucwords()` up until that change.
