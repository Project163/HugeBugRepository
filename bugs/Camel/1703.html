Title

After restarting route a camel-quartz component fires right away (and it shouldn't)

Description

I tried to keep the fire interval as low as possible, but it has to be large enough to demonstrate the problem. So I went down to 0/5 (fire every five sec), but it's more obvious with 0/10 or 0/15, the trigger starts firing at the wrong time.
