Title

Fsync after closing WAL during Roll Over

Description
When we've rolled over to new segment, written a few entries to the new wal, and crashed before syncing the old wal, we end up losing the opids not synced. This results in an opid gap between the segments. Fix this by fsyncing the wal after closing the old segment.
