- Actually, I realize that I _think_ this will only happen if you enable one of the experimental plugins

* babel-plugin-proposal-unicode-property-regex
* babel-plugin-transform-dotall-regex

so maybe leave those disabled for this file for now?

Edit: Oh only one of them is experimental still, so you could also explicitly exclude `transform-dotall-regex` in your `preset-env` config.
