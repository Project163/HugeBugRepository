Title

PolicyWrap does not properly support ExecuteAndCapture()

Description
`ExecuteAndCapture/Async()` does not currently give meaningful results on `PolicyWrap`.

`.ExecuteAndCapture(...)` is _intended_ to tell you whether policy execution was successful or failed, defined as:

+ `OutcomeType.Success`: no exception was thrown and return result (if any) was not specified to be handled by the policy
+ `OutcomeType.Failure`: an exception was thrown, or a result was returned which was handled by the policy; subdividing into:
  - `FaultType.ResultHandledByThisPolicy`
  - `FaultType.ExceptionHandledByThisPolicy`
  - `FaultType.UnhandledException`: 

With PolicyWrap, this raises conceptual questions:

+ A PolicyWrap instance does not itself define any exceptions or results to handle; only its constituent policies do.  
+ A PolicyWrap instance may comprise multiple constituent policies, each of which may (by design) handle different exceptions or results.

In that context, we need to define what it means to say a given exception or result was or was not handled by the `PolicyWrap`.

---

## PROPOSAL

`.ExecuteAndCapture(...)` on a `PolicyWrap` should indicate that a given exception or result was handled by the `PolicyWrap` if the outermost policy in the `PolicyWrap` handled it.
