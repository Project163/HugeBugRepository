Title

Description

<a class="addthis_button_google_plusone_badge" g:plusone:size="smallbadge"  g:plusone:href="[URL] causes XmlPullParser to throw the following exception:

     at org.apache.wicket.markup.parser.filter.RootMarkupFilter.nextElement(RootMarkupFilter.java:58)
