Title

Resetting form while field array is hidden shows strange behavior

Description
**Describe the bug**
When resetting a form while field array is hidden, the fields are not reset correctly and have wrong values.

**To Reproduce**
Steps to reproduce the behavior:
1. Open the codesandbox below
2. Click `Show form` button
3. Click `Hide form` button
4. Click `Reset` button
5. Click `Show form` button again

**Codesandbox link (Required)**
[URL]

**Expected behavior**
After the step 5, I expected the form had two fields `John Doe` and `Bill Luo`, but it has only one field `John Luo`.
When the form is submitted, only `Jon Doe` is sent.

After resetting again or if reset before step 2, it works fine.

It worked as expected in react-hook-form < 5.3.0.

It will work if I put `useFieldArray` outside of `MyForm` component
