 PS: For the dependency issue, there is indeed a better way. The pom.xml included the synapse-xxx JARs in an incorrect way (maybe related to some bug in older versions the maven-war-plugin). The right way is to declare them as dependencies. Maven will then take care of the rest (i.e. including the transitive dependencies).
