Title

[Unix] VideoMode when rotated incorrect

Description
VideoMode does not take into consideration rotation.

For example, I am running X at 1600x1200, but xrandr rotated 90 deg, so the effective resolution is 1200x1600.

Impl just needs updated to respect current and possible rotation resolutions (x/y flipped on all if supported by xrandr).
