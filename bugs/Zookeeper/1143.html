Title

Protocol desynchronization after Connect for (some) old clients

Description
Some old clients experience a protocol synchronization after receiving a  ConnectResponse  from the server. 

 This started happening with   ZOOKEEPER-4492  , "Merge readOnly field into ConnectRequest and Response," which writes overlong responses to clients which do not know about the  readOnly  flag. 

 (One example of such a client is ZooKeeper's own C client library prior to version 3.5!)
Discussion
- Also related:  ClickHouse ticket, "Incompatibility with Zookeeper 3.9"  whose custom   client was updated by  patch "Add support for read-only mode in ZooKeeper" .
