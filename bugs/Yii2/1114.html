Title

Replace deprecated strftime() with date() in YiiRequirementChecker.php

Description
As of PHP 8.1.0, the strftime() function has been deprecated and will be removed in future versions. To ensure compatibility and avoid warnings, we should replace all instances of strftime() with the modern and recommended date() function.
