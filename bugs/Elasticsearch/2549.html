Title

Range queries fail on runtime fields

Description
Range queries currently fail on runtime fields with a `NullPointerException` when not both lower and upper bound are specified:

```
java.lang.NullPointerException: null
	at java.util.Objects.requireNonNull(Objects.java:222) ~[?:?]
	at org.elasticsearch.xpack.runtimefields.mapper.KeywordScriptFieldType.rangeQuery(KeywordScriptFieldType.java:142) ~[?:?]
	at org.elasticsearch.xpack.runtimefields.mapper.AbstractScriptFieldType.rangeQuery(AbstractScriptFieldType.java:113) ~[?:?]
	at org.elasticsearch.index.query.RangeQueryBuilder.doToQuery(RangeQueryBuilder.java:514) ~[elasticsearch-8.0.0-SNAPSHOT.jar:8.0.0-SNAPSHOT]
... [Log Snipped] ...
	at java.util.concurrent.ThreadPoolExecutor$Worker.run(ThreadPoolExecutor.java:630) [?:?]
	at java.lang.Thread.run(Thread.java:832) [?:?]
```

Regular range queries have no such limitation, and can specify either a lower or an upper bound, not requiring both.
