- Hi  jark , I wonder under what circumstances will the close method be called before the open method?
- If the job is failed before the window operator initialized, this might happen.  hailong wang
- You are right. More correctly, the dispose method is called before the open method when job is failed before the operator initialized. For the close method is invoked when job finished or is canceled, and when job is failed, the dispose method is invoked.
