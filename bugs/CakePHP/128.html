Title

3.0 Cake doesn't look in plugin's src/ folder

Description
Cake still looks for controllers etc. in the old-fashioned way in the plugins' root dir, rather than in their designated src/ folder. Baking a new plugin and a controller for it will cause Cake to fail with a missing Controller error, whereas moving the Controller/ dir out of src/ and into the plugin root will solve it. The Template/ dir is mysteriously exempt from this problem and works as intended when located in the plugin src/ dir.
