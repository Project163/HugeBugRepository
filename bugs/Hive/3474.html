Discussion
- Here's the problem. Two of the optimization rules, ConvertJoinMapJoin and RemoveDynamicPruningBySize, are put into stats dependent optimizations and run together in TezCompiler. As ConvertJoinMapJoin is removing the reduce sink, it may also have removed a dynamic partition pruning operator chain. However, RemoveDynamicPruningBySize doesn't know this and still tries to traverse that removed chain, which ends up with no parent being found.
- The fix will be to remember the removed chain if any in ConverJoinMapJoin, and skip traversing that in RemoveDynamicPruningBySize.
