Title

Fix eclipse-warning problems

Description
The  ant eclipse-warning  target has accumulated some failures again. Locally, I'm seeing 3 errors on 2.2, 5 errors on 3.0, 23 errors on 3.9, and 33 errors on trunk. 

 Depending on the amount of overlap between these errors, it may make sense to split this into sub-issues.
