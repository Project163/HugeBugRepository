 There is a Calc(select= [$0 AS id, $2 AS ts, 1 AS row_num] , where= [($1 = 'online')] ) between Exchange(distribution=[hash [id] ]) and Deduplicate(keep= [LastRow] , key= [$0] , order= [ROWTIME] ). According to  FLINK-9528  and  FLINK-16887 , after the FlinkChangelogModeInferenceProgram, the UpdateKindTrait of Deduplicate(keep= [LastRow] , key= [$0] , order= [ROWTIME] ) will be come  [BEFORE_AND_AFTER] , that the Deduplicate operator will produce message with UPDATE_BEFORE message. As a result, the sortedRightState will become: 
 [-U, 0, online, 1970-01-01 08:00:00.000]  
# [-U, 0, offline, 1970-01-01 08:00:00.010] , it is filtered 
 [+U, 0, online, 1970-01-01 08:00:00.020]  

 Finally, only the  [+U, 0, online, 1970-01-01 08:00:00.020]  is joined, that's exactly equal to the actual output. 

 Overall, the solution would be not to push the filters in above filters into right table when it's a temporal join.
