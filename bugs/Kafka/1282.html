- Thanks  arae , from the stack trace I think it is a valid issue, such that when you have multiple instances co-locating on the same machine, they may try to  cleanup  concurrently on the same directory. However, the listing of the dirs and the clearing of them are not atomic, so it is possible that  NoSuchFileException  is thrown indicating that another instance has just deleted this folder. 

 I have just uploaded a quick fix to this JIRA, could you apply it and let me know if it resolves your scenario?
