While the types are slightly different in 1.4 and v2 the behaviour is the same.

In particular only when using tuple_() with an in_ statement are the types properly propagated to the right sice, in all other cases the types of the bingparams seems interfered from the value.

I would expect that the bindparam types are either always derived from the value or always derived from the left side

I've not tested all combination so there may be other behaviours too.
