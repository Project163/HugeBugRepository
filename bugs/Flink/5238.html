- The exception actually showed that everything works as expected. However, the message is currently checked by identity, while apparently, we just received an equal message (I feel that this is connected to the inlining of Java's string pool). So the proper fix is to just check for equality. 

 While doing so, I have also switched to use Flink's internal ExceptionUtils and made it more lenient in this way.
