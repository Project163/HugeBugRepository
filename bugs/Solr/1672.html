Title

Raise default ZooKeeper session timeout to 30 seconds from 15 seconds.

Description
Feedback has shown that 30 seconds is a more friendly default.
Discussion
- I tried to keep this down, but over time I've come to think it makes less and less sense. The benefits are not worth the initial experience for those that don't have things setup and running in a fast environment. The cost of an expiration can be quite high as well, and increases as your index size increases, as it can easily trigger replication recoveries.
