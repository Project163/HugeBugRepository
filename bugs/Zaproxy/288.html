Title

CONNECT requests not shown in History tab

Description
**Describe the bug**
The CONNECT requests are not shown in the History tab.

**To Reproduce**
Steps to reproduce the behaviour:
1. Enable Options > Display > Show (local) CONNECT requests;
2. Quick Start > Manual Explore > `[URL]
3. Note in the History tab that there's no CONNECT request.

**Expected behaviour**
The CONNECT requests are shown in the History tab, like in ZAP 2.9.0.

**Software versions**
 - ZAP: 2.10.0
