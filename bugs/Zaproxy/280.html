Title

Extensions not stopped nor destroyed when add-on is uninstalled

Description
**Describe the bug**
The extensions are not stopped nor destroyed when the corresponding add-on is uninstalled, preventing the extensions from properly terminate or clean up their resources.

**To Reproduce**
Steps to reproduce the behaviour:
1. Fuzz a message (e.g. send 10k empty payloads).
2. While the fuzzer is running uninstall the Fuzz add-on;
3. Note that in the log there are some errors (as the fuzzer extension was not destroyed).

**Expected behaviour**
The extension should be stopped and destroyed when the corresponding add-on is uninstalled.

**Software versions**
 - ZAP: 2.10.0
