Title

CylinderGeometry

Description
Sorry, I don't know in which revision the bug occurred but it is present in r58, and I believe it was present in r56.

THREE.UV was replaced with THREE.Vector2 but lines 113 and 140 access .u rather than .x.

console.log("uv2.u", uv2.u); 
outputs
uv2.u undefined

and generally fubars the texture
Discussion
- Actually, the cylinder end-caps when textured do not look much better even with this bug fixed.

Perhaps the end-faces could have `materialIndex = 2` (i.e., support an alternate texture), and the end-cap UVs could be set so as to map the circular end-cap to an n-gon with center located at the texture center.
- Indeed, I believe this is a general problem when mapping a rectangular texture to a triangle. Still, radial segment counts greater than 20 or so generally look better, depending on the texture, as you intimate.

An alternative to using an alternate texture would be to sub-divide the end caps.
