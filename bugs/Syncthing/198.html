Discussion
- To me this looks like your PC went to sleep in some state which caused us to panic, hence marking as a bug.
- I think the deadlock detection doesn't handle sleep very well. We start two goroutines that race to completion - one that grabs and releases the lock and one that sleeps for an amount of time. If the lock takes two seconds to acquire and the computer goes to sleep for two hours after one second, then when waking up it will seem like we've tried to acquire the lock for two hours and we'll panic.

Probably this should be retired. It was useful for a while when we had deadlock bugs in the model, but lately I've only seen this triggered by hardware problems and now this...
