Title

Identity casts of ROW types are not detected

Description
The CastRule infrastructure has issues detecting identity casts of complex types such as ROW. Although the planner should eliminate most identity casts, they sometimes still slip through, which is why detecting them in the code generation is beneficial for performance. Esp. on heavily nested ROW structured, which could lead to a significant performance regression otherwise.
