### Describe the Bug

`next.config.js` has `incrementalCacheHandlerPath` that used to work prior to [Link: v13.4.13-canary.4] and was supposedly broken by [Link: this commit]

As far as I can see custom cache handler is being imported with require without accessing .default field. 
Previously used `require.resolve` worked fine. 
As a result the default export was not accessed and custom cache could not be created:
