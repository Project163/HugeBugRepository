Title

Syncthing v1.27.11 on desktop claims there are Local Additions for all folders from Android v1.27.10

Description
### What happened?

After upgrading to Syncthing v1.27.11 on my desktop, it claims all my receive-only folders have Local Additions. These are being synced from Syncthing on Android v1.27.10. Reverting to v1.27.10 on desktop and disabling upgrades fixes the issue. Comparing hashes of the files shows that they never changed.

### Syncthing version

v1.27.11

### Platform & operating system

Windows x64
