- The patch for 2.1 is  here  and the patch for 2.2 is  here . 

 The problem comes from the fact that the column index used by the  Comparator  performing the sort is the one of the fetched columns and not the one of the  ResultSet  columns.  
In the case where no  Selector  is used the two indexes are the same. It is not always the case when some  Selectors  are used. 

 The patches make sure than the index used to sort the columns are the proper ones. 
They also fix  UntypedResultSet  which was providing access to the columns used for ordering.
