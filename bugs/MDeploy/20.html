Title

Uldis
- The plugin responsible for packaging the artifact ( maven-jar-plugin , maven-war-plugin , etc) will attach the file(s) to the  MavenProject  instance, so that the following plugins can  install  or  deploy  these files. 
It is not the  maven-install-plugin  or  maven-deploy-plugin  who has the knowlegde which files are packaged and attached. 
For that reason  deploy:deploy  won't work, but  jar:jar deploy:deploy  probably would.
