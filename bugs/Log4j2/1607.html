- Hi  twisterrob , 

 Thank you for reporting the issue. The problem you are having is not with the  highlight  pattern, but the  style  pattern: 
 
	 the  highlight  pattern separates the keywords with white space: e.g. " INFO=bright white ", 
	 the  style  pattern separates the keywords with commas: e.g. " bright, white ". 
 

 I agree that this is inconsistent, but it is easily fixable: instead of just using commas as separators in the  style  pattern, we can use commas  or  white space. This way we take care of backward compatibility and bring some consistency to the two patterns. Can you open a new JIRA ticket for this and perhaps submit a PR?
