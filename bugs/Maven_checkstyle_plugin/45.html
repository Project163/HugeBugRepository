Title

Unconfigured checkstyle plugin duplicates entries in aggregated report

Description

	 I deleted the  reportSets  configuration from the  pom.xml . 
	 I modified  src/main/java/org/MyClass.java  to actually have errors.

Discussion
- As a workaround you may configure a  reportSet  section in your pom for projects having no modules: 
  
  <plugin> 
     <groupId> org.apache.maven.plugins </groupId> 
     <artifactId> maven-checkstyle-plugin </artifactId> 
     <version> 2.8 </version> 
     <reportSets> 
         <reportSet> 
             <reports> 
                 <report> checkstyle </report> 
             </reports> 
         </reportSet> 
     </reportSets> 
 </plugin>
