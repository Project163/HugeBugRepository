Discussion
- This affects master only and has a simple fix:  BufferSetterGetterUtils  needs case statements for  TimestampNTZType  that replicates what is done for  TimestampType . 

 Edit: Also, the filtering of  TimestampNTZType   here in AggregationQuerySuite  should be removed so that  TimestampNTZType  gets tested.
