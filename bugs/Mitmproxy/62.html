I'm doing more research on this, I have to be doing something wrong managing Python dependencies.  Now I have it reproduced in OS X too.
- So @dstufft has pointed out that this is caused by having conflicting upper bounds on the version of enum34, plus pip's lack of a proper resolver.

In part to resolve this I've just pushed hyper-h2 version 2.1.2 that lifts our upper bound on enum34 to v2.0. Grabbing that should resolve the problem in this case.
