Title

DocsPage - Unexpected scroll behavior

Description
**Describe the bug**
Clicking on the first story in the navigation scrolls you to the bottom of the first story's wrapper div.  This is particularly noticeable when the story's height exceeds the height of the window.

**To Reproduce**
Steps to reproduce the behavior:

1. Go to [URL]
2. Note in the left nav, you are on the first item in the "DocsPage" group, "With Subtitle."
3. Note that you are scrolled to the bottom of that story's wrapper div.

**Expected behavior**
You are not scrolled at all when visiting the first story but remain at the top of the view.
