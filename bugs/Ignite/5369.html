Title

Add authorization of Ignite Cluster Node stop/restart operations.

Description
Before  [URL] IgniteCluster#restartNodes()/stopNodes() operations were authorized by the name of the internal tasks that is used during their execution - org.apache.ignite.internal.cluster.IgniteKillTask. 

 After  [URL] has been resolved, authorization of internal tasks is no longer performed. So IgniteCluster#restartNodes()/stopNodes()  operations are not authorized at all. 

 We must to fix it.
