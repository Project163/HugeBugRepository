Title

CanvasRenderer: setClearColor doesn't work, but setClearColorHex does

Description
As the title says. I was using setClearColor with WebGLRenderer and everything worked great. I swapped it for CanvasRenderer and it was clearing the screen with white. I tried setting things like autoClear and calling clear(), but then I realize the screen IS being cleared, so I swapped instances of setClearColor for setClearColorHex and now the clear is happening at my color.
Discussion
- Do you mind setting up a [Link: jsfiddle] that reproduces the issue?
- Actually it appears the issue might stem from omitting the opacity parameter. WebGLRenderer has no problem if you do something like:

```
setClearColor(new THREE.Color( ));
```

whereas the CanvasRenderer either clears the screen with transparent (apparently; since it shows the page background color) or doesn't clear it at all. It requires its second parameter, opacity, and when you pass it an opacity, suddenly things work. Maybe you could just add `|| 1` to lines 126 and 135 of CanvasRenderer.js?
