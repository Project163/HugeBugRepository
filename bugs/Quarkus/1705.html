Title

@TransientReference results in @Dispose method being called multiple times

Description
### Describe the bug

If I use TransientReference the injected bean gets disposed more than once.

### Expected behavior

Produced beans should be disposed once and only once.

### Actual behavior

Produced beans are disposed of multiple times if TransientReference is used.

### How to Reproduce?

1. Have a produce method require a different TransientReference bean that has a dispose method.
2. Watch that bean get disposed multiple times.
