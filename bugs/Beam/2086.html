Discussion
- Commented on the PR: "streaming mode" is a specific concept for some runners, but is not a concept for a pipeline. This looks like a bug in the DataflowRunner overrides.
- Specifically, it looks like  StreamingFnApiCreateOverrideFactory  gets rid of all the  Create  transforms, but then  StreamingShardedWriteFactory  adds one back. The overrides cannot be applied in this order.
