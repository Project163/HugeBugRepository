Discussion
- This looks similar to   BEAM-4280   regarding the fnapi DirectObserver class which now times out if the phase is not reached to avoid deadlock.
- This is different from the other issue because we are not making the call from a grpc thread.  The executor for grpc client callbacks is unlimited so those blocking doesn't explain why the rpc never becomes ready. 
The rpc may be unready because the channel is either not accepting or has too much data queued.  It is unclear why that happened but we should timeout when waiting (to stream deadline for example) instead of blocking forever.
