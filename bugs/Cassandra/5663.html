Title

org.apache.cassandra.metrics:type=Streaming,name=ActiveOutboundStreams is always zero

Description
org.apache.cassandra.metrics:type=Streaming,name=TotalIncomingBytes  and  org.apache.cassandra.metrics:type=Streaming,name=TotalOutgoingBytes  work fine but  org.apache.cassandra.metrics:type=Streaming,name=ActiveOutboundStreams  is always 0.
Discussion
- While working on   CASSANDRA-16190  , I discovered that  StreamingMetrics.ActiveOutboundStreams  was not used by any part of the code. This appears to be the case since at least  2.1 . I think it is time that we deprecate it in order to remove it in the future.
- I created a PR to mark the metric as deprecated  here .
