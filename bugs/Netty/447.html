Title

Removing ByteToMessageCodec from pipeline does not post remaining bytes to channel

Description
Netty Version: 5.0.0.Alpha2

Context:
I have a line-based protocol that upgrades to a binary protocol. After removing the line decoder (extending ByteToMessageCodec) during the upgrade, I lost buffered data from the line decoder.

Removing a ByteToMessageDecoder from a channel pipeline will fire the buffered bytes in that decoder into the channel to make them available to other channel handlers. Removing a ByteToMessageCodec, however, does not do the same and instead silently discards the data.The javadoc of ByteToMessageCodec states that it should behave like a combination of ByteToMessageDecoder and MessageToByteEncoder, which it does not in this case.
