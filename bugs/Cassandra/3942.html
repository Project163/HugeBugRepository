Title

Index transaction dealing with cleanup doesn't notify indexes of partition deletion

Description
SecondaryIndexManager.CleanupGCTransaction  doesn't notify registered indexers of the partition level deletion on commit. Indexers  are  notified of each individual row removal caused by the partition delete, but also informing them of the top level delete would enable them to make potential optimizations during cleanup.
Discussion
- This is an oversight that was missed in   CASSANDRA-9459  ,  CleanupGCTransaction::onPartitionDeletion  is being called by  SIM::deletePartition , it just then doesn't do anything with the supplied  DeletionTime .  

 There is however, a complication in that the partition passed to  SIM::deletePartition  isn't technically deleted by a cleanup (it's just not included in the rewritten sstables), so we have to fabricate a  DeletionTime  to pass on to the  Indexers .
