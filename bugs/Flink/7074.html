- Hi  mapohl  , do you think it's suitable to let CI judge whether it's timed out for this test? Currently it use  
  
 
@Timeout(value = 60)  
  
 to judge the timed out status.
- I guess, you're right. The test timed out after 60 seconds (which is where the log warning about the  InterruptedException  occurred). But the test itself continues because we're not forwarding the interrupt exception within the sleep call (see  YarnTestBase:744 ) and succeeds eventually. 

 Removing the timeout would have helped here to avoid test instabilities. Even though, it would have been interesting to investigate why the job submission takes that long. Unfortunately, the build artifacts are gone already.
