- Committed as     to 3.0 and merged into trunk.
- Hmmm I guess this possibly breaks consistency in repair streams + MVs 
In StreamReceiveTasks mutations are applied without commitlog because cf is flushed at the end. But MVs are not flushed. 

 Either: 
Also flush all MVs at the end of the stream task - it is not said that this is actually required for all MVs as we do not know where view replica updates eventually go. 

 Or: 
Enable commitlog for view replica updates even if base table does not commit log writes.
