Discussion
- I've changed the line in src/CMakeLists.txt

`if ("${CMAKE_C_SIMULATE_ID}" STREQUAL "MSVC")`

to

`if ("${CMAKE_C_COMPILER_FRONTEND_VARIANT}" STREQUAL "MSVC")`

and it compiles now.

But not sure if it broke the clang-cl support.
Also `CMAKE_C_COMPILER_FRONTEND_VARIANT` supported only on CMake 3.15+ I think.
- Hey all, I've just had this exact same issue. The aforementioned fix works for me as well (change `CMAKE_C_SIMULATE_ID` to `CMAKE_C_COMPILER_FRONTEND_VARIANT`)
