Title

LottieTask listener triggers twice if a cached url is retrieved a second time

Description
### [Link: Repo case]

### Problem: Listeners attached to a LottieTask trigger twice if the url is already cached from a previous request.

#### Case 1
**Steps To Reproduce**
Steps to reproduce the behavior:

- Click on the "Click me a" Button
- Click on the "Click me a" Button (second time)
- Click on the "Click me a" Button (third time)
- Resulting logs:
```
18:01:46.059  D  Click me a clicked
18:01:46.373  D  onResult 0
18:01:48.504  D  Click me a clicked
18:01:48.505  D  onResult 1
18:01:48.534  D  onResult 1 // second/duplicate invocation of the callback
18:02:29.887  D  Click me a clicked
18:02:29.888  D  onResult 2
18:02:29.899  D  onResult 2 // second/duplicate invocation of the callback
```
#### Case 2

This behaviour becomes a problem if the callback is wrapped within a `suspendCancellableCoroutine` ([Link: here])
- Click on the "Click me b" Button
- App crashes because the continuation is resumed twice
- Resulting logs:
```
18:03:31.383  D  Click me b clicked
18:03:31.411  D  Shutting down VM
18:03:31.415  E  FATAL EXCEPTION: main
                 Process: com.airbnb.lottie.issues.compose, PID: 28440
                 java.lang.IllegalStateException: Already resumed, but proposed with update LottieComposition:
                 	Layer 6 Outlines
                 		Shapes:
```
