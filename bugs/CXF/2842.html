Title

parameter state may contain spaces, it must be urlencoded in response.

Description
In org.apache.cxf.rs.security.oauth2.services.AbstractImplicitGrantService the state parameter is not queryEncoded. Since it could contain spaces (as of RFC 6749  [URL] it should be queryEncoded in method finalizeResponse.
Discussion
- I tried to avoid decoding the request parameters in the 1st place but it can introduce the side-effects with the way redirect and audience URIs are validated and is quite sensitive, so for now it is indeed simpler to follow your suggestion. I'll need to have a closer look at it later on too, thanks
