Title

sf::Rect does not properly detect intersections for negative width/height

Description
An sf::Rect does not properly calculate intersection when width/height are negative. For example, 
sf::Rect(0,0,10,10) does not properly intersect with sf::Rect(15,15,-10,-10) but does properly intersect with sf::Rect(5,5,10,10).
