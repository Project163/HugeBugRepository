Title

[Crash] OBS will crash while renaming scene source whose related scene has been deleted.

Description
WIN-10 64
OBS.25.0.1
**100% reproduced**

**Steps:**
1. add scene1 and scene2
2. switch to scene2
3. add scene source into scene2 depond on scene1
4. delete scene1 from scene list
5. click F2 for entering renaming scene source
6. click enter to exit renaming
7. crashed 100%

In step (4), there are two ways to delete scene: del hotkey and menu.
Crash always reproduce by the first way.
