Discussion
- Can you attach a WSDL which reproduces the error?
- This is due to an inconsistency in the Class-Path attribute in MANIFEST.MF of cxf-manifest.jar and the Jars in $CXF_HOME/lib: 

 The entry jakarta.jws-api-1.1.1.jar in the Class-Path is not present in the lib-directory. But jakarta.jws-api-2.1.0.jar is contained in the lib-directory. 

 Copying  jakarta.jws-api-2.1.0.jar to  jakarta.jws-api-1.1.1.jar in the lib-directory fixes the error on startup of wsdl2java.
