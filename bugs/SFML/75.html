Title

FindSFML.cmake does not add udev to SFML_DEPENDENCIES

Description
I tried to link SFML statically and my executable shows linking errors about udev. The _udev-dev_ package is installed. Since it's not mentioned in [Link: FindSFML.cmake], I think we should add it.

Discussion
- Hmm... yes, this does seem like a problem. The original author of the joystick implementation that makes use of udev seems to have forgotten to add it as a dependency to FindSFML.cmake. The addition needed to fix this shouldn't be too complicated, but it won't make it in before 2.2.
- Why? I'd assume it's a pretty minor change and easy to test, since it's a "fix or break less often" kind of issue? So even if a fix won't  fix it everywhere, it won't get worse just due to that.
