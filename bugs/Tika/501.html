Title

(PPT/PPTX) Missing date/time in text content.

Description
Missing date/time text in text content for PPT and PPTX files. 

 The date and time are missing from the text content.  This occurs when one chooses the following with MS-PowerPoint 2010: 
1) "Insert" 
2) "Date & Time" 
3) "Update automatically" 
4) save to PPT or PPTX
Discussion
- I wonder if "update automatically" Date/Time objects don't have their texts saved in PPT/PPTX files.
