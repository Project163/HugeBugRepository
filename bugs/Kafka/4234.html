Title

Wrong placement of WARN log for truncation below HWM

Description
ReplicaFetcherThread#truncate  has a useful  WARN log  when a follower is truncating its log segment below its HWM. 

 This is of particular help when debugging data loss scenarios like those described in the motivation for  KIP-966 . 

 Unfortunately, a refactoring in  PR 5608  moved the check to  after  the truncation, which may modify the HWM. As a result, the log may not be emitted even though the log is truncated below the HWM
