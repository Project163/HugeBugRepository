Title

Turn off bitmap indexing when map-side aggregation is turned off

Description
Simply adding the CLUSTER BY clause on the ROW_OFFSET does not work with a GROUP BY clause, causing a SemanticException when trying to compile the the index builder task. Based on conversation with John Sichi, for now we will just turn off this feature.
