Title

NetBSD has non-standard libGL soname

Description
Installing glfw on NetBSD/amd64 -current I have noticed that libGL.so
have the version number hardcoded in glfw/glx_context.c:158 (while here
I have "libGL.so.2").

Is there any reason for that or could *soname be changed to just "libGL.so"
(instead of "libGL.so.1")? (it is just a one-line change or maybe
two-lines change if also Cygwin can be affected and so I am not
attaching a patch... if it is needed please let me know!)

I have changed it and tested with mupdf-gl and can confirm that getting
rid of the .1 fixes the problem that I reproduced with mupdf-gl.

Thank you!
Ciao,
L.
