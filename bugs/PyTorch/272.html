Discussion
- I tested this case with both Colab nightly (2.9.0.dev20250906+cpu) and local build (2.8.0+cu126).

Using:

`import torch, torch.nn.functional as F

xq = torch._empty_affine_quantized((8, 6, 0, 7), scale=0.07663, zero_point=85, dtype=torch.quint8)
F.interpolate(xq, size=(4, 9), mode='nearest')
`

and also with scale_factor=2.0, the calls succeed and return valid quantized outputs.

The earlier traceback (ValueError: either size or scale_factor should be defined) was expected, since calling interpolate(x) without size or scale_factor is invalid.

So at the moment I can’t reproduce a crash on quantized tensors with zero-sized dims. behavior matches float path.

Could you confirm if there’s another repro case or environment where it still crashes? If not, this may be safe to close.
