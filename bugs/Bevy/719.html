Title

y axis flipped when reading delta from Drag pointer

Description
## Bevy version 15.2

## What you did

I spawned an observer watching an entity for a `Drag` trigger. On that trigger, the observer adds the `delta` to the entity's position.

## What went wrong

The entity follows the mouse cursor along the x axis but goes the opposite direction on the y axis. This reminds me of a similar situation in an earlier version of Bevy with the window axis and the Bevy axis. Easy to get around but thought I would mention it anyway.
Discussion
- I would say this is expected behaviour. The delta is in screen coordinates, not world coordinates. You have a "similar" problem if you zoom the camera in - the delta will not match correctly with the world units, since the delta is in screen coordinates (which don't care about the camera).

I think documenting this on the picking events would be good. Note we can't convert the screen coordinates into world coordinates since that depends on what you're doing. In 2D it's fairly unambiguous, but in 3D, we don't know what depth to pick. That could require raycasting and stuff. But if we can make it clear that this is in screen coordinates (and also mention something to convert screen to world coordinates), I think that'll be good enough.
- I agree that the fix here is docs.
- Ran into the same issue and was super confused. Especially because the X axis are the same.
