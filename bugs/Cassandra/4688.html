- The initial stacktrace from the description shows that NativePRNG tries do write the seed into /dev/urandom. Any values written there will not reset the seed system wide (which would be funny), but simply provide a bit of additional entropy. SHA1RPNG seems to work differently though, but the  javadoc  isn't very clear either what exactly to expect.
