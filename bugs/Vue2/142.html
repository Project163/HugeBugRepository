Title

Pitfalls of Vue dependency detection may cause redundant dependencies

Description
### Version
2.4.2

### Reproduction link
[Link]

### Steps to reproduce
click the button，then App component will update twice！

### What is expected?
App component updates only once

### What is actually happening?
App component updates twice

---
