Title

Form->input() type=>'time' generates incorrect values for times of "12:xx" using a 12 hour format

Description
Cakephp 2.4.2

Sample code in view:

```
echo $this->Form->input('Event.time', array(
   'type' => 'time',
   'interval' => 15,
   'selected' => '00:00:00',
 ));
... [Log Snipped] ...
   'selected' => '13:15:00',
 ));
```

Expect to see prepopulated:
12 00 am
12 00 pm
12 15 pm
1 15 pm

Actually see:
12 00 am
12 00 am  <-- incorrect
12 15 am  <-- incorrect
1 15 pm

It works as expected in CakePHP 1.3.
