Title

logical error with term counting in org.apache.mahout.vectorizer.DictionaryVectorizer

Description
when using mahout lda for topic modeling, creating vectors from SequenceFile is essential, (refer to  [URL] but when the --minSupport was set a little bit larger, I found the term counting not right — there is a logical error at org.apache.mahout.vectorizer.DictionaryVectorizer.java:line 335 
    job.setCombinerClass(TermCountReducer.class); 

 Now turn to line 41 at org.apache.mahout.vectorizer.term.TermCountReducer.java 
    if (sum >= minSupport)  
{
      context.write(key, new LongWritable(sum));
    }

 so some terms would be filtered at Combiner even though they actually could pass through, absolutely this is not what we've expected.
Discussion
- I agree that looks like an error. Who's the author – can I just turn off the combiner? 
Or we can leave the combiner, but when used as such, it can only sum and not filter.
- Agree with U! 
And turn off the combiner does work! That's the easiest way, even though this may hurt the efficiency, but not serious.
- Bleh, easy enough I just fixed it by separating out the combiner.
