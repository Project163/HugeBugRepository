Title

docs-infra: class/interface overview styling has changed

Description
The styling of the overview for classes, interfaces etc has changed.

* The linenums are now in a strong colour that makes them stand out harshly (they used to be light grey - and in fact in v5 were not there at all).
* The spacing between overview items has increased, which gives too much space for what is essentially just a navigation list.
