Title

Remove the redundant blocking queue from DeployingTasksBenchmarkBase

Description
We find that the  BlockingQueue  that used to preserve  TaskDeploymentDescriptors  which are never used later. Since a  TaskDeploymentDescriptor  would cost massive heap memory, it may introduce unnecessary garbage collection and make the result of benchmarks related to deployment unstable. Therefore, we think it's better to remove the redundant blocking queue in  DeployingTasksBenchmarkBase .
