Title

Make yii\i18n\Formatter::formatNumber method protected or public

Description
### What steps will reproduce the problem?
Override class [Link: Formatter] and override [Link: asShortSize] method and try to call [Link: formatNumber] from your asShortSize method.
### What is the expected result?
[Link: formatNumber] method to be protected or even public.
### What do you get instead?

I have extended Formatter class and overrided [Link: asShortSize] method.
