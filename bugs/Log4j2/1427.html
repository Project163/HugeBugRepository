Discussion
- Any updates on this please?
- The HtmlLayout in Log4j 2 was implemented to match what the Log4j 1 HtmlLayout did. It prints the timestamp as the number of milliseconds since the JVM started. I have no idea why it was decided to do that.  There currently is no way to customize the format.  

 I am not aware that any of the current committers use this layout nor do we get many questions about it so I wouldn't expect this to be a high priority for any of us to work on. 

 Patches are always welcome.
