- We have space in S3AFilesystem.addDeprecatedKeys() for precisely this kind of thing. Should be backported to branch-2 and 3.2.x 

 3.2 will switch from sequential to random the first time you do a backward seek, so when reading ORC & Parquet files, if they read the footer and then seek back to the first columns they actually want to scan, it'll kick off the switch. the random mode lets you do that without wasting the first read (if the pos() after the footer read < (len(file) -fs.s3a.readahead.range) we'll abort and switched to ranged GETs; if less than that then it'll just scan to EOF and recycle that HTTPS connection. 

 But: Hive now pushes down the (pruned) footer to the workers during a query, so you don't get that backward seek. So we do need it to say "random IO"
