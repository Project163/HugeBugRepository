Discussion
- Unfortunately your 7zip file is too big for Commons Compress to handle. 

 The maximum unpack size of the encorded header that Commons Compress can handle is 2,147,483,647 bytes, which is 2GB. In your case, the unpack size 2,416,988,886, which is larger than the data Commons Compress can handle. 

 Commons Compress will try to read all the unpack data of encorded header into a byte array, which has a size limit of 2GB. 

 Actually it's a little surprised for me that there are 7zip archives with headers that a larger than 2GB. For most cases, the headers are very small, as they are only the meta data of the compressed info, not the compressed file data. 

 I can not provide any good suggestions. Maybe you can try to divide the large 7z archive into 2 small ones(I'm not talking about creating split 7z archive, it's not helping)?
