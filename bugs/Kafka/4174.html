Title

ConsumerNetworkThread.cleanup() throws NullPointerException if initializeResources() previously failed

Description
The  ConsumerNetworkThread  initializes its  NetworkClientDelegate  and  RequestManagers  objects in  initializeResources() . If either object fails its initialization, an error is thrown, causing the  AsyncKafkaConsumer  to close. During the consumer close process, the  ConsumerNetworkThread  calls its  cleanup()  method, but a  NullPointerException  will be thrown when attempts to dereference either the  NetworkClientDelegate  and/or  RequestManagers  objects.
