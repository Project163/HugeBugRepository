Discussion
- I haven't been able to reproduce this at all unfortunately. 

 It definitely looks like a cell was null but I could not see why it would be null, perhaps an issue with the bounds? What do we mean exactly by "short reads"?
- Short reads are when a query comes in with LIMIT N, and each replica returns N rows to the coordinator, but after merging the results together the coordinator has less than N rows left because of non-shared tombstones.  At that point the coordinator needs to request extra rows from the replicas to satisfy the original query. 

 Look for  short reads  in StorageProxy.
