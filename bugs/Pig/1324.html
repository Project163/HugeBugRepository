Title

PigCombiner creating log spam

Description
"Aliases being processed per job phase" is logged a lot of times even though it is in setup because Combiners can be called multiple times in both map and reduce by MR framework (  HADOOP-3226  ). With Hadoop 2.0 since logs go to hdfs this is inefficient.
Discussion
- No new test added. But ran TestCombiner with -Dhadoopversion=23 

 find target | xargs grep -H "Aliases being processed" | grep Combiner | wc -l 

 Before the patch it was 34 and after the patch it was 5.  

 As for the usefulness of cutting down on this log statement - in the case reported by one of our users, one map task alone had the same log line repeated 1001 times and the job had 32K maps.
