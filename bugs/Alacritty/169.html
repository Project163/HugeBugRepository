Title

Glyphs sometimes cut off (need to draw background in single pass)

Description
I'm getting a visual glitch which causes the right half of certain characters to be missing. Restarting alacritty fixes it.

It happens when working with han characters, which are double width. I haven't been able to figure out the exact situation when it occurs. It seems to occur most often after displaying a lot of characters, so perhaps it's due to a cache getting filled up.

In this image, the first han character on the screen is okay, and the last three are truncated.

[Image: half-characters]
Discussion
- I believe this is due to us not drawing the entire background in a single pass. When the first glyph cache gets filled up, Alacritty starts batching draw calls by texture. This is supported by what you've described
