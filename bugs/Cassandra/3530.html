- Pushed a fix  here  

 This actually fixes the problem twice, just for good measure. First, it cancels any in progress compactions when dropping, which seems useful anyway and should prevent us ever calling unmarkCompacting during the invalid period. 

 Second, it removes the extra pre-unmark check, and just calls unreference..() in the event we're invalid, since there doesn't appear to be a good reason to do it twice, and the second check is safer than the first.
- Does this mean we will now block upto 1 minute for compaction to finish when we drop?  We shouldn't do that.
