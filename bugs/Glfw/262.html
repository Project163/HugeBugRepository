Title

Swap interval ignored when going to fullscreen

Description
OS and version: Windows 10 64-bit (10.0.15063 Build 15063)
Gfx card: GTX1080 driver: 385.41
Release or commit: master ( )
Test: "boing" project in examples (64-bit)

The app starts with vsync turned on and hits 60fps, but becomes much higher framerate after going fullscreen (alt + enter). 

I've found two separate workarounds:
- Put another glfwSwapInterval(1) after glfwSetWindowMonitor on line 262
- ~~Move the original glfwSwapInterval(1) before glfwMakeContextCurrent on line 644~~

Edit: ignore the second workaround, it's invalid to do that
Discussion
- Successfully reproduced on Windows 10 with 342.01 running a G 105M. Unable to reproduce with 385.41 (same driver version as OP) on Windows 7.

I assumed that this was because of the `GLFW_DONT_CARE` and now always manually call another `glfwSwapInterval` after switching to fullscreen - which is fine right?
