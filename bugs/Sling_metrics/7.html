Title

The MetricsService keeps stale metrics after bundles that have registered metrics are removed

Description
The  MetricsService  keeps stale metrics after bundles that have registered metrics are removed. Although the respective metrics are removed from the  MetricsRegistry , the  MetricsService  internally caches the metrics and does not remove them once metric mappings are removed.
