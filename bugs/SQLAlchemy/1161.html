Title

asyncpg connections not being invalidated upon asyncio exceptions

Description
**Describe the bug**
An asyncio exception (e.g. `CancelledError`) experienced during a call to asyncpg's `execute` method does not result in the invalidation of the dbapi connection. Instead, the connection is returned to the connection pool in a dodgy state. Subsequent executions then pick up the connection and attempt to execute transactions as nested transactions.
