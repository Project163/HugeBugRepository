Title

Resume after Behind-Live-Window-Exception issues

Description
**Crashes on versions 2.13.+**


**Steps to Reproduce** :

1) Use HLS live stream URI as a data source. (Ex : [URL]
2) Attach a PlaybackStatsListener to the exoplayer.
3) Reproducing `BehindLiveWindowException` will crash the app.  (Pause the live stream for more than twice the live window duration and then resume the video to reproduce `BehindLiveWindowException`)

Works fine if I don't attach the PlaybackStatsListener by removing the below line
`player?.addAnalyticsListener(playbackStatsListener)`

- I can't reproduce with just playing this stream, adding a `PlaybackStatsListener` and causing a `BehindLiveWindowException`.

Could you let us know what your app is doing once the `BehindLiveWindowExceptions` is reported?
