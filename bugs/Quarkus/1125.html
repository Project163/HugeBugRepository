Title

Resteasy Reactive: InputStream is closed too early

Description
## Describe the bug
There seems to be a regression from 2.0.0.Alpha3 to 2.0.0.CR1 where now in CR1 a ContainerRequestFilter that tries to read the inputstream some time after suspending the ContainerRequestContext will end up reading it forever and eventually timing out as the underlying stream is constantly returning saying it has bytes available as its hitting this code in org.jboss.resteasy.reactive.server.vertx.VertxInputStream and input1 is null:

[Image: image]

It thus returns the value of the content length header and the stream reader ends up trying forever to read as it thinks there are always more bytes available.

### Expected behavior
The stream should remain readable even after a suspend has been done. 

### Actual behavior
In CR1 the stream becomes unusable soon after a suspend is called


## To Reproduce

I don't have a reproducer and was hoping the cause could be found as it is specifically happening due to some changes from Alpha3 to CR1

Discussion
- Interesting... I can't imagine what changed from Alpha3 that could have caused this, but I'll take a close look on Monday
- I managed to reproduce it and it turns out that the issue comes in due to our filter removing the content-length header from the request context as we proxy the request and instead of creating new headers, we just removed ones that cannot be proxied. I worked around this by simply creating a copy of the headers and removing the ones that cannot be proxied and all works fine. Not sure what changed that triggered this though.
- ... [Middle Discussions Snipped for Brevity] ...
- I took a look at this and here are my conclusions:

* I could reproduce the issue with `CR1` and verified that with `Alpha3` there was no problem.
* I could **not** verify that the `content-type` header has any effect
* I could verify that the `available` fix does make the problem go away.

After a lot of debugging, I came to conclusion that the difference in behavior between `CR1` and `Alpha3` comes down to [URL]
It seems that because we are returning `-1` for `VertxInputStream#available`, the `java.io.PushbackInputStream#available` (which is being used in `io.netty.handler.stream.ChunkedStream`) is returning a positive number and thus the attempt to read data from the stream never end.

I will open a PR changing the `-1` value, but it will need to be verified by folks with more Netty knowledge than I possess
- Weird that removing the content-length header does not create the issue. I
was sure it did on the demo project.

Anyway, I can confirm that simply doing this in our real environment does
create the issue. Perhaps some weird edge case kicking in:
