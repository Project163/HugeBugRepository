Title

Topic can not be recreated after it is deleted

Description

Discussion
- Topic deleting is async – thus, you will need to put a retry logic into place in case the topic did not get deleted yet.
- Today the most safe way to confirm a topic is deleted is via ZK directly, but that may not be accessible for all Kafka users. Using the kafka-topics tool would also be better as it queries the source of truth than the broker caches which may be stale.
