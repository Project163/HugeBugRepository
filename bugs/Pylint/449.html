There are two issues here. First one is the `invalid-name`. I presume it's emitted because we expect all globals to be constants, rather than classes, and the default pattern for constants is upper case letters.
The second issue seems more important as it seems that the global analysis cannot find what the global declaration of `ClassToBeDecorated` is pointing at.
