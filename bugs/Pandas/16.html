Title

.append drops MultiIndex, .join not supported for MultiIndex

Description
You are probably already aware, but .append and .join are not yet fully functional with MultiIndex.  
1. .append turns the index into numbers

1. .join errors out

1. both .join and .append are fine when you are lining the data up with MultiIndex, rather than creating an expanded MultiIndex.
         A       B  
   bar  one  0.5341  0.5299
    two -0.3412  0.8686
   baz  one  0.6352  1.835 
    two -0.4022 -0.2686
