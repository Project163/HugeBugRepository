Title

Google Team Drive support for emptying trash

Description
Hi,

Google Team Drives behaves differently than regular Drive, it does not support the API method that seems to be used by 'rclone cleanup' [1] and thus it does not empty the trash for Team Drives when configured.

I believe Google intended to not allow a simple method to delete all data in a team drive. I think maybe rclone could obtain list of files in the trash bin for a team drive and execute a delete individually to get around this limitation by Google?

Right now emptying the trash on a Google Team Drive using 'rclone cleanup' does not seem to work.
