Discussion
- FWIW, I think this may be a real bug that has been present for years. It's not clear what our semantics are with respect to max mutation size - seemingly we should reject them entirely at the client level, but for some reason we do not. We do not seem to declare our behaviour clearly anywhere. However, historically we could not durably record them in the commit log, so we should presumably not be recording them in the hints file either - but we are, and have been for a long time. 

 Probably what we should really do is to default to a much larger max mutation size, reject client operations that exceed it entirely, and have the commit log support any size of mutation (by just allocating a dedicated segment). Perhaps we already do, in fact, and that explains this divergence? I'm not sure, I'm out of date.
- seemingly we should reject them entirely at the client level, but for some reason we do not.  

 I agree here since the oversized mutations can still affect the heap, which with G1 can cause a Humongous Allocation and wreck it.  This is why I reduced the native frame size in   CASSANDRA-16886  , but this is also why we should try not to record them as much as possible, so I'm not fond of the idea of allowing any size since we'll surely be allocating at some point.
