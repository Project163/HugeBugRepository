Title

Cannot use `session.execute(...).unique().scalar_one()`

Description
**Describe the bug**
Calling `.scalar_one()` after unique raises an error. Using `.scalars().one()` works instead

**Expected behavior**
`.scalar_one()` to be a synonym for `.scalars().one()`
