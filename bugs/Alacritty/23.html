Title

Cursor disappears on first column in vim

Description
Moving the cursor down in vim while on the first column causes the cursor to disappear until moved to the second column of a non-empty line. This is on macOS or Linux, vim or neovim, on the scrollback branch at  .

Reproduce: `vim <(echo 'one\ntwo\n\nthree')` and scroll down.
