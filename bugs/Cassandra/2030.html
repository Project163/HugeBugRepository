Title

Avoid serializing to byte[] on commitlog append

Description
We used to avoid re-serializing RowMutations by caching the byte[] that we read off the wire.  We don't do that anymore since we fixed MessagingService to not create intermediate byte[].  So we should serialize the mutation directly to the commitlog.
Discussion
- 1.2 version just gets rid of the byte[] caching, since it's never actually re-used.
- 2.0 version adds ByteBufferOutputStream and ChecksummedOutputStream to get rid of byte[] serialization entirely.  Also fixes mutation-length checksumming to include the entire length, not just the first eight bits.
- +1 for both.
