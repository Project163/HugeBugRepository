Path history isn't preserved for keys in mutations

Description

     When a key of a mutation was a traversal it was not being integrated to the parent traversal and tracked in the Parameters. Works now with: 

     ```text 
    gremlin> g.withSideEffect('k','name').addV('person').property(select('k'),'stephen')
    ``` 
