### Expected Behavior

Expecting OBS to try reconnect obediently according to settings after the remote target closes its socket when using a custom outbound SRT-stream.

### Current Behavior

After opening an SRT stream out from OBS and then closing the listening socket on the receiving software to test the reconnect capability, we experienced a crash after a few attempts. Issue is repeatable, but crash behavior differs depending on system. On my main machine, the exception is not caught by OBS, and WER kicks in. On a virtual machine (Win 10) on the same system, OBS detects the exception and creates a crash log. On one laptop I tried with Win 11 the reconnect did not render a crash at all, and on a second laptop OBS just shut down with neither it nor windows giving any notification about an exception, no OBS crash log, and nothing notable in the regular OBS log.

I attached WinDBG to OBS running on my main machine and caught the exception. By the looks of it, there is a double free happening somewhere. I'm not experienced in C++ though, so I see little chance of finding the actual cause, but perhaps the following output from WinDBG is helpful for someone else.
