Title

Plural of virus isn't really viri

Description
I'll quote chapter and verse from a dictionary if required, though I don't think it should be necessary. lib/Cake/Utility/Inflector.php in the latest 2.x build (apparently in 3.x too) includes "virus" as one of the words that has its "us" changed to "i". It should be left out of the regexp (line 50, starting with '/(alumn|bacill|cact' etc) since the correct English plural of virus is viruses, so will be caught by the catch-all on line 51.
