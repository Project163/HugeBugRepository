Title

Upload speed per file is not accurate

Description
As described in the title the upload speeds per file vary a lot, while I get a pretty constant overall upload speed displayed, for example `Transferred:   1.305 GBytes (584.462 kBytes/s)`

Here is a screenshot of the problem:
[Image: bildschirmfoto vom 2016-12-24 11-04-38]

As you can see the sum of the individual speeds does not result in the overall speed. Also the ETA is alternating alot and probably also not very accurate.

Maybe the smothing algorithm could be improved.
Discussion
- It's not an overall speed but an average speed over the last 5 minutes. The individual speeds is at that exact moment. If your transfer speeds wildly fluctuate as yours appear to be doing then the differences you highlight are to be expected.
- This is why I asked to also use average speeds for individual speeds for better ETAs and better information.
