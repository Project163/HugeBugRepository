Title

"Ignore Patterns" panel Save/Close buttons not always accessible

Description
I have SyncThing Android 0.6.5 / 0.11.13 on an HTC One (M8, a smartphone) and a Nexus 7 (2013, a 7 inch tablet) which have resolutions of 1920x1080 and 1920x1200 respectively. When I open a shared folder's "Ignore Patterns" panel, the Save and Close buttons are rendered off-screen, and I cannot scroll/zoom the pane so that they are accessible. As it stands, the Ignore Patterns functionality can't be managed via the Web UI on these sorts of devices.

I also tried this via web browsers and could access the buttons on the Nexus 7, but not the HTC One.
