Title

static::$stack misused in yii\base\Widget of begin() and end functions

Description
### What steps will reproduce the problem?
When re-declare the public static property  `$stack` in the sub class of `yii\base\Widget`,
the exception of 
```php
            throw new InvalidCallException('Expecting end() of ' . get_class($widget) . ', found ' . get_called_class());
```
would not trigger correctly due to `static` keyword before `$stack`.
This happens when two widgets begin and end not correctly.
e.g.
Both WidgetA and WidgetB are instance of yii\base\widget.
ether WidgetA or WidgetB redeclared the $stack;

```php
 
 
 
 
```
### What is the expected result?
Even though the public static property $stack redeclared in the widget,
the exception could also be thrown.

### What do you get instead?
when $stack is pushed and popped, use self::$stack instead of static$stack
