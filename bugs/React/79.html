When `isLazy=false`, the tree should render just fine. I was able to render `depth=13` without _much_ trouble. But when `isLazy=true`, it renders fine till `depth=5`. when `depth=6`, it hits a some kind of cliff and locks the CPU/GPU up for MANY seconds. with `depth=7`, chrome has to be force closed. 

We fire a SHIT TON of `retrySuspendedRoot` calls after the initial burst of js eval. Even though the calls take <0.5μs, react appears to yield back to the thread for 10μs, and then call it again.
