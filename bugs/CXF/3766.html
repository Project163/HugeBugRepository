Title

Connection Leak in HttpClientWrappedOutputStream due to Unreleased Resources on IOException

Description
When the `close` method of the parent class `org.apache.cxf.transport.http.HTTPConduit.WrappedOutputStream` is called, a timeout occurs at line 1420, resulting in an `IOException`. This exception is caught at line 1428 and subsequently rethrown to the upper layer. 

 The upper layer, specifically the `close` method in `org.apache.cxf.transport.http.HttpClientHTTPConduit.HttpClientWrappedOutputStream`, does not properly handle the exception. As a result, the `pout` resource (which corresponds to the socket for writing) is not released, leading to a connection leak.
