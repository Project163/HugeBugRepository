Title

IllegalArgumentException from ThreadPoolExecutor. in surefire.booterclient.ForkStarter.runSuitesForkPerTestSet

Description
In some Maven project, e.g.  maven-core , run: 

  
 $ mvn -DforkMode=perthread -e test
....

	at java.util.concurrent.ThreadPoolExecutor.<init>(ThreadPoolExecutor.java:1275)
	at java.util.concurrent.ThreadPoolExecutor.<init>(ThreadPoolExecutor.java:1163)

 Fix seems to be to specify  -DthreadCount=<something> , but the mojo should say so politely.
