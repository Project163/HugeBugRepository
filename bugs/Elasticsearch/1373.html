Title

BulkProcessor can release too many permits on its semaphore

Description
BulkProcessor uses a semaphore to control the level of concurrency on its underlying client. Prior to acquiring a permit on this semaphore, BulkProcessor#execute invokes BulkProcessor.Listener#beforeBulk. If this method throws, then BulkProcessor#execute releases a permit on its semaphore. This is a mistake, and can lead to more than the intended number of threads having a permit on the semaphore.
