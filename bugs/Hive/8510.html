Title

ThreadPool in HS2 over HTTP should respect the customized ThreadFactory

Description
In Jetty, ExecutorThreadPool will override the ThreadFactory of ThreadPoolExecutor even though the ThreadPoolExecutor has already initialized the ThreadFactory,  
  
 
_executor.setThreadFactory( this ::newThread);  
  
 Need to ignore such action as we have injected a ThreadFactory into the ThreadPoolExecutor.
