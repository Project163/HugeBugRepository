Title

Visual bug: Selected radio button backgrounds have too much right padding

Description
# Summary

The grayish background oval indicating a selected `st.radio` label has too much padding on the right hand side by a few pixels. Here's an example:

[Image: image]

(Notice how the background rounded rectangle extends further to the right past "Notion" than it does to the left of the selection toggle!)

This is also true when you do it the main area rather than the sidebar.

## Expected behavior

I would expect both the left and right side to have the same amount of padding.

## Is this a regression?

Unknown

- OS version: `MacOS Big Sur 11.3 (20E232)`
