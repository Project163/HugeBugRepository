Title

the failover provider can block if the transport drops while establishing the new connection.

Description
When restablishing a connection after it drops, if the new connections transport drops during the process of opening the connection the failover provider can block awaiting its completion which will never come. This was observed agaisnt an instance of qpidd being stopped and started every few seconds. Its not clear why the transport dropped immediately after being created, but it should be handled, and the incopminete connection open aborted so another further attempts can take place.
