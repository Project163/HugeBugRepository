Title

1.5.5 broke playing videos captured on recent iphones

Description
I have an android app that's playing videos from a server that are uploaded from Android and ios devices.  Recently many of the videos uploaded from the ios devices stopped working in exoplayer.  I was able to track down the the version of the Android app where things went wrong, it was when I updated exoplayer from 1.5.4 to 1.5.5 (1.5.6 doesn't work either).  Reverting back to 1.5.4 resolved the issue.

The problem seems to be videos captured on  iPhone 6+'s and some iPhone 6's (In our experience all the videos on 6+'s failed to play, and some 6's but not others).  Sorry, but I have absolutely no idea how the videos are different.  They appeared to encoded the same way.

I'm happy to supply some examples of the videos that don't play.
