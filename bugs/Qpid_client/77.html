Title

make JmsInitialContextFactory and JmsConnectionFactory defaulting behaviour consistent

Description
The JmsInitialContextFactory defaults the remoteUri property of the JmsConnectionFactory instances it creates if none is specified, whereas instantiating the JmsConnectionFactory directly without specifying the property leaves it without any. The behaviour should be made consistent between the two either by moving the default into the factory itself or by removing it.
