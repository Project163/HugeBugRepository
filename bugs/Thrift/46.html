Title

Erlang Makefile not compatible with Solaris

Description
The sed lines in lib/erl/src/Makefile weren't happy on solaris. Here's a replacement for them: 

 MODULES = $(shell find . -name *.erl | sed 's:^ ./::' | sed 's/\.erl//') 
MODULES_STRING_LIST = $(shell find . -name *.erl | sed 's:^\./:":' | sed 's/\.erl/",/')
