Title

AIOOBE in Segment#toString

Description
Segment#toString  doesn't account for correcting the offset of a record in a segment in the case where the total segment size is less than the maximal segment size. This usually leads to an AIOOBE at the point where the root refs / blob refs are listed.
Discussion
- Turns out the the toString method did not used the correct offset into the buffer in case  Buffer.position() > 0 .
