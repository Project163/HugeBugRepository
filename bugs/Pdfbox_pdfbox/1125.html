- The two error messages should be downgraded to warnings, they mean that a length parameter in a stream is incorrect, and PDFBox then tries to read the stream until it finds "endstream" instead of relying on the length parameter. This is because your PDF is malformed, somehow all newlines were transformed in 0d 0a. Either the creator messed up, or they were transferred in ascii mode from a unix to a non unix system.
