Title

New Linux sandbox sometimes hangs with defunct child process

Description
The PID1 process of the new Linux sandbox sometimes doesn't seem to notice that its child process died and gets stuck with this stack trace:

linux-sandbox.cc:
#0    in __libc_waitpid (pid=9124, stat_loc= , options=0) at ../sysdeps/unix/sysv/linux/waitpid.c:31
#1    in WaitForPid1() ()
#2    in main ()

linux-sandbox-pid1.cc:
#0  do_sigwait (sig= , set= ) at ../nptl/sysdeps/unix/sysv/linux/../../../../../sysdeps/unix/sysv/linux/sigwait.c:63
#1  __GI___sigwait (set= , sig= ) at ../nptl/sysdeps/unix/sysv/linux/../../../../../sysdeps/unix/sysv/linux/sigwait.c:93
#2    in WaitForChild() ()
#3    in Pid1Main(void*) ()
#4    in clone () at ../sysdeps/unix/sysv/linux/x86_64/clone.S:111

Running "kill -CHLD" on the pid1 process lets it continue and the build completes successfully. It seems to happen randomly, about once in three "bazel build //src:bazel" clean builds.
