Title

Configured SSL Context on Undertow component not honored by camel-rest-swagger

Description
Say you have: 
  
 
    <camel:sslContextParameters id= "sslContextParameters" >
        <camel:trustManagers>
            <camel:keyStore password= "redhat123"  type= "JKS"  ... />
        </camel:trustManagers>
    </camel:sslContextParameters>

.. and ..

    <bean id= "myundertow"  class= "org.apache.camel.component.undertow.UndertowComponent" >
        <property name= "sslContextParameters"  value= "#{sslContextParameters}" />
    </bean>

.. and ..

    <to id= "_to1"  pattern= "InOut"  uri=" rest -swagger:swagger.json#getMembersList?basePath=/membersweb/ rest /v2&amp;componentName=myundertow&amp;host=https: //myapp.demolab.local:443”/>
