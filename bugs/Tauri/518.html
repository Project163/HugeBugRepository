Title

[bug] tauri v2 bundler treat `gnueabi` and `guneabihf` all as `armhf`

Description
### Describe the bug

When producing .rpm or .deb packages in Tauri v2, the bundler incorrectly treats both gnueabi (soft-float) and gnueabihf (hard-float) targets as armhf. As a result, packages for both soft-float (armel) and hard-float (armhf) platforms are incorrectly generated as armhf, leading to potential runtime issues on devices that expect armel.
