Title

external_integration_test is occasionally flaky

Description
I saw `test_moving_build_file` in `external_integration_test` fail once. All the other tests cases passed, and that one passed on the next try.

I've only seen this once in many runs of the tests, so it doesn't seem to happen very often. Seems like a pretty low priority to fix, but good to keep track of.
