Title

Missing method setConditonClasses in \yii\db\QueryBuilder

Description
### What steps will reproduce the problem?
Try to configure query builder as mentioned [Link: here] with [Link: custom codition]

### What is the expected result?
To don't throw Unknown Property – yii\base\UnknownPropertyException
Setting unknown property: yii\db\mssql\QueryBuilder::conditionClasses

### What do you get instead?
throw Unknown Property – yii\base\UnknownPropertyException
Setting unknown property: yii\db\mssql\QueryBuilder::conditionClasses

### Additional info
The method setConditonClasses in \yii\db\QueryBuilder is missing.
The property $conditionClasses is protected but it could be public, because the init() method in QueryBuilder overrides the initial value (see line 125: $this->conditionClasses = array_merge($this->defaultConditionClasses(), $this->conditionClasses);)
