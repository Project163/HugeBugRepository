Title

Custom Fact execution is skipped after moving discovery/testcase to portable assembly

Description
In [Link: this change] I moved the discovery and test case classes from a desktop-platform assembly to the same portable assembly that defines the Fact attributes themselves. But after that change, the `[SkippableFact]` test cases are no longer executed. They are discovered, as I verified using the debugger with xunit.console.exe. But they are not executed, nor are they listed in the console output or Test Explorer in VS.
