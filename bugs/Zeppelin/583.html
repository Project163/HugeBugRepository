- kavinkumarks  I think you may be confused interpreter group and interpreter. For your understanding, you should know the 'InterpreterGroup' and 'interpreter'. What people say SparkInterpreter means all of Spark*Interpreter including SparkInterpreter, SparkSqlInterpreter and so on. Thus correctly should we call "InterpreterGroup". but in general, people calls it to SparkInterpreter becuase SparkInterpreter is a default interpreter of SparkInterpreter group. The default Interpreter what I told you means actually default 'InterpreterGroup' name. If you use `%spark`, that means internally `%spark.spark`. Thus, your first screenshot means `md` is a default interpreter gorup in that note, and the second screenshot means Alluxio interpreter group has only one interpreter in this group name alluxio. Thus if you use `%alluxio`, that means as same as `%alluxio.alluxio'. Thus that's the normal situation. BTW, I also agree to have it confused.
