Title

Geometry.mergeVertices, Face4 and MeshFaceMaterial

Description
Geometries having Face4's with MeshFaceMaterial assigned will throw a "map error" after merging vertices with Geometry.mergeVertices.

Reason is obvious: mergeVertices triangulates the Face4 to two Face3's but doesn't copy over Face4.materialIndex.

So after mergeVertices the geometry contains Face3's without Face3.materialIndex set. 
Then of course MeshFaceMaterial can't find its material.
