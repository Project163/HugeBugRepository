Title

ClassNode isRecord and isSealed not useful before AST transformation

Description
The new to Groovy 4 methods  ClassNode#isRecord  and  ClassNode#isSealed  do not work for a node that has not yet been processed by the record/sealed transform or in some cases  ResolveVisitor .  And they do not work for a node that is a reference to a record or sealed type. 

 For these reasons, I request that the methods be moved to  ClassNodeUtils  with some extra notes or made private within classgen somewhere.
Discussion
- Definitely the documentation should be expanded. 

 We do have other methods which are impacted by AST transforms, e.g.  MethodNode#isPublic  might change (and hence doesn't work  correctly  in earlier phases) when processing  @PackageScope . 

 Having said that, there are counter examples where we've moved such methods to other classes like  Traits#isTrait . 

 In this case however, you'd typically call  isRecord  just before calling  getRecordComponents  and  isSealed  just before calling  getPermittedSubclasses , it seems a little weird that you'd want that functionality split across  ClassNode  and  ClassNodeUtils .
- Since  getRecordComponents()  and  getPermittedSubclasses()  safely return non-null results, is there any reason to check  isRecord()  or  isSealed()  first?  The accessors can function on their own. 

 True,  MethodNode#isPublic  can be affected by  groovy.transform.PackageScope .  But the accessor reports on the modifier.  For the most part, modifiers are available from a quite early compiler phase.  And for a decompiled class, it works just fine. 

 My preference is not to split functionality between ClassNode and ClassNodeUtils, but instead to remove isRecord and isSealed from the public API until their need is clear and their lifecycle concerns are more fully understood.
