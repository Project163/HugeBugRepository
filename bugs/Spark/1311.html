- I think you're just running out of memory on your driver. It fails to have enough memory to copy and serialize two data structures, syn0Global and syn1Global which contain (vocab size * vector length) floats. With a default vector length of 100, and 10M vocab, that's at least 8GB of RAM, and the default for the driver isn't nearly that big. 

 I think this is just a matter of increasing your driver memory. I imagine you will need 16GB+
