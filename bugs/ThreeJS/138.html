- @jarethgk

It sounds like your instance of `THREE.WebGLRenderer` does not have shadows enabled, but you have them enabled on a point light in your scene. When shadowing is disabled in `THREE.WebGLRenderer`, `shadow.matrix` will be null. Admittedly, the code in `refreshUniformsShadow()` should do a proper check either for enabled shadows or at the very least should check if `shadow.matrix` is null. 

I am not familiar with how Clara.io works - how easy would it be to verify that your renderer has shadowing enabled?
