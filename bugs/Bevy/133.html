Discussion
- After a first incorrect impression that it's behaving correctly, I quickly realized that Bevy should be able to run without a camera. 

Then I remembered that #3635 was merged not so long ago, and tested your example on that commit and a commit just prior to that. The conclusion is that #3635 introduces this bug (well, "introduces" is a strong word, it's just that Bevy behaves as expected without this change).

I'll investigate further into this.
- This happens because even after deletion of the camera entity, there is still an `ActiveCamera` resource present for some reason.
