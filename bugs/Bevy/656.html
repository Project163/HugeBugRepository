Title

Unable to compile certain bevy crates directly, unless an executor is defined separately for `bevy_tasks`

Description
Hi! While writing some of my linting PRs, I seem to have come across an odd problem with `bevy_tasks`.

Specifically, it seems that certain crates rely on `bevy_tasks`, but then prevent `bevy_tasks` from having any executor features enabled, causing certain executor types to not be defined.

I was able to observe this while trying to compile the `bevy_state` and `bevy_hierarchy` crates directly (using `--package` to select them specifically), but it's possible there may be other bevy crates that do the same.
