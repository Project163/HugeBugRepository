Title

Bindy does not handle separator at the end of quoted field, appends next field

Description
Address fields quite often contain comma at the end of an addressLine1 field.  Example - "PROBLEM SOLVER","8506 SIX FORKS ROAD,","SUITE 104","RALEIGH","NC","27615","US" 
Bindy will append next field to such one shifting all others to the left.
Discussion
- Test project with suggested patch(for version 2.10.3).  
Situations with separator at the beginning of field still requires attention. Though in real life such use cases are probably rare.
