Title

[YSQL] Restore of backup created with pg_hint_plan enabled fails

Description
Jira Link: [Link: DB-714]

The customer encountered an issue where they created a backup of a Universe with pg_hint_plan enabled, then when they tried to restore the backup to a clean Universe, the restore failed because the index hints_norm_and_app was in the schema but not in the snapshot.

They worked around this by enabling pg_hint_plan on the restore target.

Our backup and restore should be improved to cleanly handle this case.
