Title

Rename DeliveryThrottle to MessageWindowSize

Description
In hedwig server, DeliveryThrottle is a little inaccurate as it suggests that the rate is being limited, rather than a outstanding message limit being reached. I think MessageWindowSize is more accurate, and is analogous to windowing in tcp, which this mechanism actually replaces.
