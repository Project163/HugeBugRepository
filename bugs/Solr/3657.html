Title

When legacyCloud=false, cores should not publish itself as DOWN on startup

Description
When we load CoreContainer, we already publish a DOWNNODE operation to ZK. Which publish all its cores as DOWN.  
Then each of its cores publishes itself as DOWN. These are wasted operations in case of legacyCloud=false.
