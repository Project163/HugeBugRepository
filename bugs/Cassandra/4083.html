Title

Invalidate legacy schema CFSs at startup

Description
ColumnFamilyStore  instances are created for legacy schema tables at startup when  SystemKeyspace  is initialized as they may be required for schema migration during upgrade. Before startup completes, the schema info for these is expunged from  system_schema , but the  CFS  instances are not invalidated, which leaves their mbeans registered and visible via nodetool & JMX.
