Discussion
- Pointed out by  pwendell , this is a Maven-vs-SBT issue. In Maven, Guava 15 is properly shaded and Guava 14.0.1 is used. However, SBT still chooses the highest version. Verified that the Maven build is not affected.
- Yes - because of this I think simply excluding guava from whatever dependency is introducing Guava 15 is fine (I think it was some of the jackson dependencies). It will coerce the SBT build to match the maven behavior.
