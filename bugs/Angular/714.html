## Current behavior
On NgConf @IgorMinar had a great talk about SEO. One of the tips was to load polyfills only when necessary and he was pointing to a solution on AIO.

After taking creadits from AIO and implementing it in our app I started to see few cases when polyfills are not loaded and I was curious why. Unfortunatelly I found same problem as we have on AIO when using IE11.

Problem is that this can be still async [URL]

Screenshots where polyfills are loaded later than main chunks:
[Image: screen shot 2018-05-02 at 13 48 23]

[Image: screen shot 2018-05-02 at 13 49 47]

After reading more about the issue I found:
`script-inserted scripts execute asynchronously in IE and WebKit, but synchronously in Opera and pre-4 Firefox.` taken from: [URL]

## Expected behavior
Polyfills should be loaded synchronously (in a blocking way).

## Minimal reproduction of the problem with instructions
I reproduced it e.g. when I went to `angular.io` and clicked on `Docs`
