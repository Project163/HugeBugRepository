Title

Websocket transport sporadically fails with corrupted data

Description
A jaxrs or jaxws service invocation over the websocket transport may sporadically fail at the server side and this can be seen as some corrupted data retrieved from the socket and passed to the service. As a result, the client may either receive the wrong response or no response.
Discussion
- This is regression from   CXF-5809   which added concurrent processing of the receive data at the server/destination side.
