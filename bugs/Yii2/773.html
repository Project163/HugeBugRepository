Title

Inflector::camelize unexpected behaviour after recent upgrade

Description
### What steps will reproduce the problem?

A bit of an edge case, but I was passing strings to the camelize function that may or may not already been in camelized format.  This changed with the latest update here  . To reproduce:

echo \yii\helpers\Inflector::camelize('HelloWorld');

### What is the expected result?

HelloWorld

### What do you get instead?

Helloworld

See commit
