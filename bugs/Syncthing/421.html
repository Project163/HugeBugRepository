Title

KCP connection attempts block the connection service

Description
Well, not the entire service, but the connection loop. Basically, if the other side isn't responding the connection attempt will take "forever" (I don't know how long, but at the very least it stops at that point for minutes). This blocks connecting to other devices and addresses.
Discussion
- Do you know where exactly it gets stuck?

I suppose if the other side advertises kcp, but is not actually listening it will block?
- ```
go
package main

import(

	fmt.Printf("%#v %#v %s\n", c, err, time.Now().Sub(n))

```

gives

```

connections.internalConn{Conn:(*tls.Conn)(nil), connType:0, priority:0} &smux.timeoutError{} 10.0083167s

So it must get stuck somewhere before that, but having checked the code, all parts seem non-blocking.
- I think it is when the other side does speak KCP, but they are not "in sync". That is, let two devices connect, restart one of them. (#4063)
- No repro on this from me for the moment. Give me a moment and I might remember how it came to be.
