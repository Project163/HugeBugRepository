Title

Executor page in SHS does not show driver

Description
This bug is sort of related to   SPARK-22836  . 

 Starting with Spark 2.2 (at least), the event logs generated by Spark do not contain a  SparkListenerBlockManagerAdded  entry for the driver. That means when applications are replayed in the SHS, the driver is not listed in the executors page.
