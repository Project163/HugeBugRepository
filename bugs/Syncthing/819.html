Title

GUI displays Syncing (100%, 0 B) when syncing byte-sized files

Description
1. Create a folder on Device A with byte-sized tiny files. In my test, I used 10,000 of < 10-byte files.
2. Share the folder with Device B.
3. While syncing, Device B displays `Syncing (100%, 0 B)`, even though the files are not `0 B`, and also not at `100%`.

[Image: image]

Also, once the synchronisation has finished, the GUI on Device A is stuck in the following state, even though the actual folders are in sync. The glitch/error does go away after pausing and unpausing the folder on both devices.

[Image: image]
