Title

Tracking page repairing has no WAL record associated with it

Description
org.apache.ignite.internal.processors.cache.persistence.tree.io.TrackingPageIO#resetCorruptFlag(long) 

   

 In order for tracking pages to work properly they should be persisted on disk, it means that failed checkpoint scenario should be supported. Tracking pages restoration has no associated WAL record so in that case binary recovery could leave them broken after a valid repair.
