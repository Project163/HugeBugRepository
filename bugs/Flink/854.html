       FLINK-3500    [test]  Fix possible instability in ExecutionGraphRestartTest 

     After restart of the execution graph, the test driver waits for the graph to assign the new executions in a loop. If `switchToRunning()` is called multiple times in this loop, the job is canceled. This could happen, if one execution had its resource assigned and called `switchToRunning()`, but the other didn't. In this case, the complete loop was retried and `switchToRunning()` was called again. 

     At least, that is the only explanation I could come up with.
