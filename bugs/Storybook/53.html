**To Reproduce**

1. Have actions, a11y and knobs installed and configured
1. Click on a story
1. select the a11y tab
1. select another story
1. See that the selected tab is now actions

**Expected behavior**
last selected tab should remain selected and active.

if i had a11y selected, it should remain selected but updated with info about newly selected story.
