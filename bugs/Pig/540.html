Discussion
- Hadoop counters are not available in local mode ( PIG-1286 ). 

 So for now I propose that, in local mode,  Pig stats output is changed to something like the following: 

  
  
Job Stats (time in seconds):
JobId  Alias Feature Outputs
job_local_0001 raw MAP_ONLY
job_local_0002 rank_sort SAMPLER
job_local_0003 rank_sort ORDER_BY Processed/user_visits_table,

Input(s):
Successfully read records from:  "Data/Raw/UserVisits.dat" 

Output(s):
Successfully stored records in:  "Processed/user_visits_table"
- Tested manually for local mode. Messages were same as proposed above. +1 for the commit. One minor suggestion is to put a line at the start saying something like: "Detected Local mode. Stats reported below may be incomplete." This will reinforce the message to users that stats reporting is not transparent across different modes (local Vs map-reduce).
- Patch committed to both trunk and 0.8 branch.
