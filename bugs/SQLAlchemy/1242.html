Title

fix SQLite native decimals

Description
we have this silly and actually embarrassing warning that SQLite does not return Decimal objects natively, and it's just wrong.  We just need to register a handler for it as illustrated at [URL] , and this is not much different from what we do for Oracle.   Lets make the switch and get rid of that warning.
