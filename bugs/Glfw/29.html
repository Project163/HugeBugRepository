Title

Windows with disabled cursor iconified by Win+D cannot be restored

Description
Steps to reproduce:
- Open a glfw window and disable the cursor.
- Press win+d (minimize all / show desktop).
- Click the glfw application on the taskbar.

Window doesn't show and it's really hard to bring back. Clicking on any other application on the taskbar instead of the glfw window results in the same thing.

This is on Windows 7.
