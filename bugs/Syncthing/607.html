Title

Systemd circular dependency prevents startup

Description
The current systemd service file prevents startup on some systems.
Since  , syncthing depends on multi-user.target to be finished. However, multiuser-target in turn depends on syncthing. That leads to a hanging systemctl start, at the very least when enabling syncthing via

`$ sudo systemctl enable syncthing@MY_USER.service`

[Link: As discussed on the forums], a revert may be necessary (or a better solution).

### Version Information

Syncthing Version: v1.1.1 from [URL]
OS Version: Ubuntu 18.04 (desktop and server seem affected)
Discussion
- I'm actually pulling this into 1.1.2 even though it's very late in the cycle. I think this is justified by 1) it being a serious issue that blocks startup of syncthing and 2) the fix being a revert to a previously tested and known good (enough) setup.
