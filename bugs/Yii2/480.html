Title

Php7 errors may be not serializable

Description
Php7 introduced \Throwable interface that covers old \Exception and new \Error types. When \Error is thrown \yii\log\Target in formatMessage() method tries to serialize it and fails. There is already workaround to avoid serializing \Exceptions and it should be expanded to cover all \Throwable.
