Discussion
- For anyone having this issue:
Add `JavascriptCore.framework` in the Build Phases -> Linking Libraries
- @witalobenicio Where is `JavascriptCore.framework` located?
- Seen already, for anyone coming after me, click on your app target then go to Build Phases -> Linking Libraries and click on the plus sign in its section, a dialogue window will appear, simply search for `JavascriptCore.framework` select it and click on add and there you go.
