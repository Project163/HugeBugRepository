Discussion
- Seems the issue is retaining the pendingLock while firing the slowConsumerAdvisory in PrefetchSubscription
- the slowConsumerAdvisory policy needs to be specified for this to occur: 
  
 <policyEntry topic= ">"  advisoryForSlowConsumers=" true  ..> 
  
 when false (the default) this issue is avoided.
