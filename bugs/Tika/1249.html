Title

Remove XMLParser from service loading in 2.x

Description
On the user list,  snagel  observed that the DcXMLParser is being called on xml for 1.27, whereas the XMLParser is being used in 2.x.  The cause of this is that we've accidentally included the XMLParser on the service loading list in 2.x.  DcXMLParser is also on the list in 2.x, but because of the sort of service loaded parsers, the XMLParser is the only one that is called.
