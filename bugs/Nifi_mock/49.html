Title

Refactor Stateless NiFi to make use of existing components/classes

Description
Stateless NiFi introduces a slew of simple implementations of interfaces. Some of these make sense, but others end up being duplicates of existing code, in order to avoid bringing in large dependencies such as nifi-framework-core. The common components need to be factored out and made use of.
