Title

GANT_HOME ending in a slash causes ClassNotFoundException

Description
Defining the GANT_HOME variable ending in a forward slash causes ClassNotFound on gant.Gant class...
Discussion

 GANT_HOME=~/lib/Java/gant/ gant  
 fails with
