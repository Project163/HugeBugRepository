Title

Native build leads to HibernateException: Unable to parse order-by fragment

Description
**Describe the bug**
Adding a `@OrderBy` annotation to a `@OneToMany` mapping leads to a exception in Hibernate with native compilation.

**Expected behavior**
Working native compilation. 

**Actual behavior**
See 'To Reproduce'
