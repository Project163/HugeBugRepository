Discussion
- I got the repro (at least for the cast), will look tomorrow
- This is by design (sortof). Cache size needs to be increased, it's almost full and too fragmented to accommodate larger-than-usual uncompressed stream that is cached in max-alloc-sized parts. Max alloc in this case is 16Mb, stream is 4-something Mb, but maximum chunk present in buddy allocator is 4Mb.  
Alternatively we could try to break stream into smaller parts for this case
