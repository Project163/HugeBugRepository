Title

[DependencyInjection] Regression? - Serialization of 'Closure' is not allowed

Description
**Symfony version(s) affected**: 4.?

**Description**  
Have just updated symfony and now getting an error "Fatal error: Uncaught Exception: Serialization of 'Closure' is not allowed in symfony\dependency-injection\Compiler\RemoveUnusedDefinitionsPass.php"

I *believe* I may be hitting the issue due to the the following change:

[URL]

3. Trying to serialize a dependency with a previous error generates due to the \Closure argument
