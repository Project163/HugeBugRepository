- The test for this should have been  CQLSSTableWriterClientTest , but was changed when we removed client mode. I've changed it back so that it is once again testing the client mode of  CQLSSTableWriter .

I don't see any users of the method  SSTableTxnWriter.create(CFMetaData, Descriptor, long, long, int, SerializationHeader)  other than  CQLSSTableWriter , and the additional  Keyspace.open()  causes many errors in  DatabaseDescriptor .
