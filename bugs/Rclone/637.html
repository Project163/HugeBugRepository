Title

Mounting union includes the root filesystem

Description
#### What is the problem you are having with rclone?
I updated to the latest beta and now my union includes the entire root filesystem and it's not mounting the remote.

This only seems to affect mounting a union, mounting a remote directly works as expected.

rclone is including the root fileystem in the union, even though it's not specified as a upstream branch. This issue only affects the recent 1.55 beta's and I believe were caused by the refactoring of the fsPath and splitPath code, see commit [URL]

Reverting to   does not have this bug and works as expected..
