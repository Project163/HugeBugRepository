Title

The NIO transport must handle the sudden and un-expected closure of keep-alive connections by external systems which may be under load

Description
When the NIO transport deals with external servers (such as JBoss), it defaults to using keep-alive connections. However, when an external system is under load, they have the freedom to abruptly close such keep-alive connections to ensure correct operation. The NIO transport needs to expect and handle this situation gracefully
