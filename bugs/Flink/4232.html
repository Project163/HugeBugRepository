- This is due to avro conflict between hive-exec and flink-shaded-hadoop-2-uber. In flink-shaded-hadoop-2-uber  org/codehaus/jackson/JsonNode  is relocated. Therefore if we load  Schema$Field  from flink-shaded-hadoop-2-uber, we'll get this NoSuchMethodError.
