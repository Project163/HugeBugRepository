Title

[MacOS] - standard dmg icon not entirely draggable

Description
Slightly annoying is the fact that in the finder, if your icon has pure transparency (like the empty space of the tauri logo) - you will have difficulty dragging it. What happens is that MacOS thinks you want to select a draggable area - instead of simply dragging. On HiDPI screens this is quite literally super annoying.

Fix: create new standard icons (with at least a 0.1% transparent background). 
Docs: add info about this "known behavior" to the identity page.
