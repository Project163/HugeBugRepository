My research of this problem.

According to [Link: this answer] of the question "Alternate of getSystemLoadAverage() for Windows?", the method  `getSystemLoadAverage`  of java.lang.management.OperatingSystemMXBean would return `-1` in `Windows`.

It'd be suggested to use `com.sun.management.OperatingSystemMXBean` and its `getSystemCpuLoad()` and `getProcessCpuLoad()` instead.
