If you skip it (+ 4 bytes for the length itself) you get to this part of the buffer:  
    
Since the first byte is 0xFF, it is being deserialized as  NodeIdMessage  even though  GridDhtPartitionsFullMessage  mandates that at this point only  AffinityTopologyVersion  should be found.
