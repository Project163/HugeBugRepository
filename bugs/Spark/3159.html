Title

Executor should kill itself when it's unable to heartbeat to the driver more than N times

Description
Sometimes, network disconnection event won't be triggered for other potential race conditions that we may not have thought of, then the executor will keep sending heartbeats to driver and won't exit. 

 We should make Executor kill itself when it's unable to heartbeat to the driver more than N times
