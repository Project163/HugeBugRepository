Title

Matching attributes for R3 bundles should be ignored

Description
R3 bundles may have matching attributes, but they should be ignored by the framework. Currently, the manifest parser throws an exception. This should be modified to just drop the attributes and log a warning for R3 bundles with attributes.
Discussion
- I committed a fix for this, which simply logs a WARNING for each unknown attribute on an R3 bundle import/export and removes them.
