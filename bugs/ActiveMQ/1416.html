Title

Patch to fix invalid XHTML in web-console's 404 and 500 pages

Description
We created a monitor that utilizes calls to the web-console to determine stuck messages.  Occasionally, requests come back 404 (because the message disappears between calls) and the monitor dies because the XHTML for that page is invalid.  This patch makes the 404 and 500 pages valid in the eyes of the w3c markup validation service.
