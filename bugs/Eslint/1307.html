Discussion
- Just out of curiosity, can you use `new Array(10)` instead? Seems equivalent to me (though I could be wrong), and I think it's more readable/idiomatic. (Let me know if I'm wrong about the equivalence.)

```js
new Array(10).map((e, i) => ++i);
```
- You definitely don't want that - that creates a sparse array (permanently slow) and then maps it to a second array. Do this:
```js
Array.from({ length: 10 }, (_, i) => i)
```
which performantly creates a single non-sparse array.
