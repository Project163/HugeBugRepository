Title

Writing INI file, keys with . become ..

Description
Here is sample input config: 
 [learningtask]  
trains.lp = 5 

 after new INIConfiguration(conf).write() it becomes: 

  [learningtask]  
trains..lp = 5 

 seems related to the escaping of keys in the ExpressionEngine
Discussion
- The problem was in INIConfiguration.write(). Here keys that might have been escaped were written to disk. The write() method has been rewritten to operate on configuration nodes directly. These nodes have the correct names. 

 Fixed in SVN in revision 1739472.
