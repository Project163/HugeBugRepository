- Its not the jms consumer that is the problem. Its the async routing engine that waits for a reply that is not coming back. Are you using any custom components.  

 And as always upgrade your Camel version to see if its fixed, eg Camel 2.8.0 is an old release.
- I didn't have time to investigate as soon as I found this issue, so I just shoved it in JIRA to keep track of it. As you suggested, it has nothing to do with the JMS consumer, but rather with the Enricher processor not treating unhandled exceptions thrown from the AggregationStrategy when the async routing engine has kicked in. On the other hand, synchronous routing is handled properly.
