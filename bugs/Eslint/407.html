Title

escape and unescape are not recognized as globals

Description
These two are defined in all browsers and in node, but are not recognized by eslint because they are exported through the `nonstandard` category of the `globals` package which isn't imported into any current environment.

Updating to `globals@8.0.0` with [Link: this change] included will remedy this.
