Title

SQL: fail to aggregate within aliased subquery

Description
The following two queries work as expected:
* `SELECT * FROM (SELECT author FROM library GROUP BY author)` and 
* `SELECT * FROM (SELECT author FROM library) AS subquery`.

However, when aliasing the subquery with agg,
* `SELECT * FROM (SELECT author FROM library GROUP BY author) AS subquery`,
