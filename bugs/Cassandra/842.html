Discussion
- Patch to avoid storing DecoratedKeys with null key (which are generated by getRangeSlice) in the key cache.
- Why not do that in cacheKey directly to be sure we don't miss places ?
- Because attempting to cache a DK that is really just a token is a semantic error that we shouldn't just paper over.  v2 adds an assert to make that clear.
