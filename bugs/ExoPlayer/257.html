Discussion
- Thanks for reporting!
- Can you confirm the check you added fixed the issue and still allowed media to play? It's not necessarily the case that there's another AAC decoder to fall back on, although on most devices there does seem to be.
- The `startsWith("HM")` check is too broad, since it incorrectly captures unrelated devices (e.g. the [Link: Haier G21], which has DEVICE set to HM-G303-W). Can you tell us what `Util.MANUFACTURER`, `Util.MODEL` and `Util.DEVICE` are set to for the device, and we'll come up with a suitably targeted rule?
