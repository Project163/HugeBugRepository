Title

Offensive hero name

Description
### Description

In the tour of heroes app (https://angular.io/tutorial) the name of the first hero is "narco". Being from Mexico I don't find it particularly nice that narcos are being referred to as heroes in this app. Our country has more than 150K deaths since 2006 due to cartel violence (https://www.cfr.org/global-conflict-tracker/conflict/criminal-violence-mexico). It feels almost like a joke to be writing an app where I have to call a Narco a Hero.
