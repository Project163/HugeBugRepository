Discussion
- The given port is where the client expected the clusters REST server to run. 

 The problem is that the scala shell was not updated for Flip6; it still starts a legacy  StandaloneClusterClient  but tries to connect with a Flip6  RestClusterClient . 

 As a workaround please add  mode: old  to  conf/flink-conf.yaml .
- Thanks  chesnay ! I can confirm that the workaround works.
