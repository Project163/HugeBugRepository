Title

Links are unreadable in the Flat Darcula theme

Description
### Describe the bug:

Link text is practically unreadable in the "Flat Darcula" theme.

For example in the addons pane.

[Image: Image]

### Steps to reproduce the behavior:

1. Change theme to "Flat Darcula"
2. Open addon manager window
