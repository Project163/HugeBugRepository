- `Entity` is an index; think of it as `u32` to simplify. `commands.entity(entity)` returns a command queue for that particular entity, where mutation commands are recorded. Therefore there's no mutation happening to the entity inside that system. Later at the end of the stage the commands are applied by Bevy automatically and the mutations occur, somewhere else.
