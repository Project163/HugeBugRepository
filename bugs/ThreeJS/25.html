Title

Normalize Directional Light Position in Canvas Renderer

Description
Directional lights in `CanvasRenderer` do not work correctly unless the user enters a light position that is of unit length.

Perhaps it would be a good idea to normalize the light position during the lighting calculation just in case -- as is done in `WebGLRenderer`.

```
amount = normal.dot( lightPosition.normalize() );
```

[URL]

I know we have a convention that direction vectors are assumed to be normalized, but this is a somewhat confusing case, as the direction is being stored in the `position` property.
Discussion
- Agreed. 

I already don't remember why I started to normalize directional lights in `WebGLRenderer` (it was something like lights working properly in scenegraph or shadow mapping), but a side-effect of not having to do explicit normalization in the application layer was positive, it's just nicer without this clutter.

And it's cheap, you do it just once per light per frame.
