Title

Full move stuck waiting for data migration

Description
A full move operation with RF=3 from nodes {A, B, C} -> {D, E, F} gets stuck. At a high level, this is what is happening:

1. Spin up three new nodes each with a master and tserver process.
2. The tserver on D heartbeats to master leader on A and sees masters {A, B, C}.
3. Issue a changeConfig to add the master on D to the quorum.
4. Issue a changeConfig to step down the master on A.
5. Master on A steps down as leader, master on D becomes the new leader.
6. The tserver on D heartbeats to what it thinks is the master leader on A, A responds saying its no longer the master leader.
7. The tserver on D round robins trying all the masters it knows {A, B, C}, never finding a leader since D was never advertised.

Attached are relevant log files from the nodes.

[Link: logs.tar.gz]

To fix this issue, the current proposal is to add a code path for follower masters to heartbeat back to tservers with the master quorum. When D then heartbeats to A, A, instead of just saying not the master leader, will say its no longer the master leader, and then advertise the new quorum of {A, B, C, D}.
Discussion
- nice work @rahuldesirazu debugging this hard to repro issue. Looks like the workaround of restarting the TServer worked fine. And the proposed fix sounds good.
- Another aspect we discussed, is that tserver should ideally create a union of the heartbeat response list with its current in-memory list. This would avoid cases that could override the config/file value, causing it to miss any masters that are in the conf file. And vice-versa, if union in-memory is superset of what is on disk (if conf file is used), then we should ideally persist it for next restart.
- @bbaddepudi : What's HB - heartbeat? Might be worth just spelling it out
