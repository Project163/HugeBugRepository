Discussion
- This DCHECK is a little aggressive as we have a case where of the tablets may not have received a change metadata op yet, but a different tablet might have already processed 2 change metadata ops and bumped up the consumer schema version twice. In such a case, the update from the lagging tablet can be ignored safely instead of DCHECK.
