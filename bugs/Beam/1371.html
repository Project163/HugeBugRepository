Title

Dataflow distribution metrics returns additional counters

Description
Dataflow changed name endings in metrics from   [MAX]  ,   [MIN]  ,   [COUNT]   and   [MEAN]   into _ MAX,  _ MIN , _ COUNT  and _ MEAN . Because of that Dataflow returns additional counter metrics with those suffixes while collecting distribution metrics. 

 To ignore counters with new names it is required to change the suffixes in  dataflow_metrics.py  here  [URL]
