Title

Add logging during and after long running BigQuery jobs

Description
Currently, we log when the export job begins, but don't clearly log when it has completed. 

 If it takes a long time (over an hour) it would also be useful to periodically log to remind the reader that the job is still running, and make it clear the pipeline isn't hung, it's just waiting.
