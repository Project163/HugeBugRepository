Title

AppClient does not respond correctly to RemoveApplication

Description
When the Master removes an application (usually due to too many executor failures), it means no future executors will be assigned to that app. Currently, the AppClient just marks the application as "disconnected", which is intended as a transient state during a period of reconnection. Thus, RemoveApplication just causes the application to enter a state where it has no executors and it doesn't die.
