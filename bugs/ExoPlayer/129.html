Title

Missing segments at the end of the period are perhaps incorrectly considered a playback failure

Description
I have a video here that "fails" near the end with a 404. It plays fine most of the video but when playing the last half minute of the video, we run into this:

```
10-02 10:52:28.514: E/BasePlayerController(25561): onLoadError: Response code: 404
```

Video URL: [URL]

The 404 comes from the 223rd video segment, which does not exist. ExoPlayer gets a 404 and then immediately signals the error to the app and closes up shop.

Running the timing numbers tells me that there should be 223 segments for all adaptation sets. The audio duration is a bit less than the duration of the others (3.989s VS 4s) but it still comes out to 223 total. In practice, however, there are only 222 segments in the video stream.

Now, the spec purist in me immediately says "Hey, DASH does not allow missing segments unless you are using `SegmentTimeline`, so fix your video". Unfortunately, this situation - uneven adaptation sets, with some being shorter than the period - is very common with VOD content produced by the various available encoders. Therefore, I would expect players to be more lenient here and ignore missing segments. This is what dash.js does, for example - it plays the segments that exist and simply ignores the ones that are missing in the end, without signaling a failure.

A different but related scenario is live video, where missing segments are a common thing that occurs when network disruptions happen between the encoder and the origin server. I have not tested ExoPlayer with regard to this scenario but maybe it is worth tackling two birds with one stone and ensuring that ExoPlayer does its best to skip past missing segments in all scenarios.
Discussion
- I faced the issue of missing segments with live content before. The ExoPlayer then performes the configured number of requests for the missing segment, and exits afterwards. It would be great to support skipping segments in that case.
