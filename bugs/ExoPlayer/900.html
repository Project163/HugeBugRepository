Title

Incorrect profile/level info reported in MediaCodecInfo

Description
### ExoPlayer Version

Older (unsupported)

### Devices that reproduce the issue

Pixel 1 running Android 10, running 2.12.3

### Devices that do not reproduce the issue

_No response_

### Reproducible in the demo app?

Not tested

### Reproduction steps

Attempt to play the manifest linked below in exoplayer. 

### Expected result

The media plays successfully, i.e chooses the AVC variant

### Actual result

The renderer crashes with a `MediaCodecVideoDecoderException` when attempting to play the HEVC variant.
After some internal searching, we've found that the `profileLevels` array generated by `MediaCodecUtil.getDecoderInfos()` includes the constant `HEVCProfileMain10`, even though the device does not support 10 bit HEVC as confirmed in [URL] 

This seems to be a bug in the support check.
