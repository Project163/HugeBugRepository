Title

JAX-RS seeOther does not work with IPv6

Description
### Describe the bug

If you access a Quarkus app on an ipv6 address on a port, like [URL] and send a Response.seeOther in your jaxrs resource, the ResponseBuilderImpl gets confused by the extra ":" charecters while trying to extract the port and fails.

### Expected behavior

ResponseBuilderImpl should correctly parse an IPv6 address

### Actual behavior

java.lang.NumberFormatException: For input string: "d014:32c:xxxx:xxxx:xxxx:xxxx:xxxx]:8080"
        at java.base@21.0.2/java.lang.Integer.parseInt(Integer.java:662)
        at java.base@21.0.2/java.lang.Integer.parseInt(Integer.java:778)
        at org.jboss.resteasy.reactive.server.jaxrs.ResponseBuilderImpl.location(ResponseBuilderImpl.java:33)
        at jakarta.ws.rs.core.Response.seeOther(Response.java:713)
