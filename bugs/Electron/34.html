Title

After .clearHistory(), .canGoBack() reports false after navigating

Description

Steps:
1. Open `BrowserWindow` to `index.html`, which navigates to google
2. Perform a search to generate history
3. In development console: `document.querySelector('webview').canGoBack()` is `true`
4. In development console: `document.querySelector('webview').clearHistory()`
5. In development console: `document.querySelector('webview').canGoBack()` is `false`
6. Click on a search result link to generate history
7. In development console: `document.querySelector('webview').canGoBack()` is `false`

Step 7, canGoBack() reports false, where it should report true.

Electron version: 1.8.2
Operating system: OSX High Sierra 10.13.1 (17B1003)
