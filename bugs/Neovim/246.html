Discussion
- [URL] may be connected. `check_abbr` shows up in the stack trace there as well.
- I got bitten a few times by this. It seems to only happen in high-disk-latency environments for me.

It seems that for some reason check_abbr gets called with the columns provided from an Insstart cursor that has a column set to MAXCOL.

Now, Insstart gets populated from curwin->w_cursor, and a quick search through the sources indicate that col may be set to that value in a bunch of different situations.

A quick and dirty fix I made to avoid hanging altogether was to check for MAXCOL in check_abbr. It's probably not the "right" fix as having a MAXCOL column seems nonsensical when we reach check_abbr, but at least I don't have to sigkill my editor.

patch
