Discussion
- I can verify this, and I suspect it's caused by the exclusion of  jaxb-core  which is a transitive dependency of  akka-camel . For some reason, the EMR connector only works when this is present but it's not present in the default Hadoop classpath.
- [URL]

 The problem was that since `2.2.11` jaxb requires to include both  jaxb-core  and  jaxb-impl  dependencies.  jaxb-impl  was not excluded and it was conflicting with versions pulled in by S3 (pre `2.2.10`). Because of splitting those dependencies, if 2.2.11 version of  jaxb-impl  was pulled in, it was missing classes defined in/moved to  jaxb-core .
