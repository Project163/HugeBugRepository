Title

Non-queued scheduling failure sometimes does not return the slot

Description
Similar to   FLINK-9908  , it can happen that in case of a non-queued scheduling failure a slot is not properly returned to the  SlotPool . The reason for the failure seems to be the exceptional completion of the  allocationFuture  in  Execution#scheduleForExecution .

       FLINK-9910   [scheduling]  Execution#scheduleForeExecution does not cancel slot future 

 
	 
	 
		 What is the purpose of the change 
	 
	 
 

     In order to properly give back an allocated slot to the SlotPool, one must not complete 
    the result future of Execution#allocateAndAssignSlotForExecution. This commit changes the 
    behaviour in Execution#scheduleForExecution accordingly. 

     This PR is based on #6384.
