## Current behavior
 

`ServerModule` of `@angular/platform-server` depends on `HttpClient` of the old `@angular/http`. 
But its `peerDependencies` doesn't include it.


## Expected behavior
 

2 options

1. Add `@angular/http` to `peerDependencies`
2. Remove the dependency on `@angular/http` from platform-server. (breaking change)
