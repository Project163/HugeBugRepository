Title

DeleteShardCmd doesn't remove all its data from Zookeeper

Description
After successful completion of  DeleteShardCmd  there's still some data in ZK under  /collections/<collectionName>  that should've been removed: 

 
	 leaders/shardX 
	 leader_elect/shardX 
	 terms/shardX
