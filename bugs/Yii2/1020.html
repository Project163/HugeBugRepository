Title

"Numbers" with leading and trailing spaces are passed by NumberValidator

Description
Possible duplicate of (unresponded) #15474

### What steps will reproduce the problem?

1. use f.ex. `[['amount'], 'number']` validation rule in model
2. validate any value with leading or trailing space

### What is the expected result?

Validation fails.

### What do you get instead?

Validation passes and a `A non well formed numeric value encountered in ...` error occurs in any calculation using _validated_ number.

### Additional info

| Q                | A
| ---------------- | ---
| Yii version      | 2.0.40
| PHP version      | 7.2.33
| Operating system | Fedora 30
