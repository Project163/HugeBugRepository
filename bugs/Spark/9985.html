Title

Inconsistent string truncation

Description
Output of truncatedString is different for the same max fields: 

  
 
truncatedString(Seq(1, 2),  "[" ,  ", " ,  "]" , 2)

truncatedString(Seq(1, 2, 3),  "[" ,  ", " ,  "]" , 2)
