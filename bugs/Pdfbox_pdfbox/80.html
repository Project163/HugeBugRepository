Title

Special characters are not correctly handled anymore when printing or exporting to image

Description
I've just upgraded PDFBox from 1.2.1 to 1.3.1 and special characters (like 'é', '°', 'à', ...) are not correctly handled anymore when printing or exporting to image. These characters are remplaced by 'Ø' or blank spaces. Everything was working flawlessly with PDFBox 1.2.1.
Discussion
- I've attached a sample pdf file with special characters for testing purposes.
- The already existing font encoding was overwritten by an AFMEncoding. 

 I fixed that issue in revision 1030082. 

 Thanks for reporting this!
