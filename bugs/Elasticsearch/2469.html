Title

Wildcard field term queries have false positive matches if they contain * or ? characters

Description
The `term` query on wildcard fields delegates to the `wildCardQuery` method to do searching but this method assumes `*` or `?` characters have special meaning. 
This means the term query `foo?` mistakenly matches `foot`, `fool` etc rather than just docs with  `foo?`
