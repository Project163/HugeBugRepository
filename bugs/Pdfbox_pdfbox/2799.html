Discussion
- The cause is  processSoftMask , it doesn't save and restore textMatrix while the softmask is processed. The softmask has text and when encountering the "ET" operator, the textMatrix is reset to null. In  processType3Stream()  we avoid this problem by saving the textMatrix and another variable. It's really surprising that this hasn't hit us all these years.
