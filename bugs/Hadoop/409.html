Title

Make source tarball use conventional name.

Description
When building binary and source tarballs, I get the following artifacts: 
Binary tarball: hadoop-0.23.0-SNAPSHOT.tar.gz  
Source tarball: hadoop-dist-0.23.0-SNAPSHOT-src.tar.gz 

 Notice the "-dist" right between "hadoop" and the version in the source tarball name.
Discussion
- Here is the tony patch doing the following: 
 
	 It removes the "-dist" in the source tarball 
	 It adds a "-bin" suffix to the binary tarball to be more aligned with other Apache artifacts
- As an exemple, here are the produced artefacts from hadoop-dist/target/: 
 rw-rw-r -  1 bruno bruno 41928210 Oct 31 18:23 hadoop-0.23.0-SNAPSHOT-bin.tar.gz 
 rw-rw-r -  1 bruno bruno 13722578 Oct 31 18:04 hadoop-0.23.0-SNAPSHOT-src.tar.gz
