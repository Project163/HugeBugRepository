Title

remnants of removed nodes remain after removal

Description
After nodes are removed from the ring and no longer appear in any of the nodes' nodetool ring output, some of the dead nodes show up in the o.a.c.net.FailureDetector SimpleStates metadata.  Also, some of the JMX stats are updating for the removed nodes (ie RecentTimeoutsPerHost and ResponsePendingTasks).
Discussion
- I believe this may be evidence of the issue I've heard reported where nodes are still trying to connect to dead IPs that were removed.  I suspect a message might be getting stuck in OTC and causing this. 

 As far as the FD goes, we definitely remove it there in Gossiper.removeEndpoint, so something must be adding it back.
- Does this happen in 1.1 as well?

- Membership lock so no one else add's the node back into the map during the remove sequence, it would be rare... +1 other than that.
