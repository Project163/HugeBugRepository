Title

pathing rewrite broke the contract that Config.get_template_directory() is supposed to be canonical source of templates. tests were missing

Description
**Describe the bug**
My dead simple flask app is broken after upgrade from 1.15.2 to 1.16.0. Previously, `flask db init` just worked and now it fails with `Error: No such template  /lib/python3.11/site-packages/alembic/templates/flask`. The template in question is located at ` /lib/python3.11/site-packages/flask_migrate/templates/flask` however, and somehow the older version of alembic was able to find it.

Apologies for a brief bug report, I am on the road right now so can't debug further. This seems to be important enough to report ASAP however.
