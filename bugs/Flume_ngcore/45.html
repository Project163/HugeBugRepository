Title

Maven deps on flume-ng-configuration module are brought in transitively instead of directly

Description
The maven dependencies on flume-ng-configuration are not brought in directly by the modules that reference configuration-related objects, such as Context. This also affects IDEs such as Netbeans, which will not auto-complete transitive dependencies.
