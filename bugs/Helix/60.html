Title

Paragraph movement doesn't work if paragraphs are separated by more than one blank line

Description
### Summary

`]p` stops at the line just above the paragraph when it has multiple blank lines before it.

### Reproduction Steps

Start with the following text and place the cursor on the first line.

```
text


after two blank lines

more text
```

Press `]p` a couple of times. It first moves to the blank line just before `after two blank lines`, but doesn't move anymore with `]p`. Manually moving the cursor to `after` and then hitting `]p` two times correctly moves the cursor first to before `more text` and then subsequently to the end of `more text`.

### Platform

Linux

### Terminal Emulator

Kitty
