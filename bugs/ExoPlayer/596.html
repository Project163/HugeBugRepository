- In terms of why this is happening: The video has an MP4 edit list that partially clips the first video frame. Our logic for edit list handling would skip this frame, and then skip forward to the next keyframe, which for this video is quite far into the video. After the change, we no longer skip a sample that's partially clipped by the edit list.
