Discussion
- The 401 appears to be on an inter-node request, which is odd since those typically use PKI for authentication.  Following up on this I added  "forwardCredentials": "true  as an authentication property in security.json, and the behavior goes away.  The behavior persists though if the "forwardCredentials" property is either "false", or missing entirely. 

 This seems like a bug in the handling of this new property.  It seems that sometimes requests are sent without PKI, even when forwardCredentials=false would indicate that they should be. 

  janhoy  do you have any guesses why this might be?  I think you were involved in the new "forwardCredentials" change.  It seems easy enough to reproduce if you get a few minutes to take a look.
