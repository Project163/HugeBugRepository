Title

slf4j-log4j binding is excluded from the dist assembly due to test scope

Description
It turns out that the slf4j-log4j binding was not properly included in the dist assembly even though it was listed in flume-ng-node as a compile scope dep. Upon investigation, it turns out that because it was listed in flume-ng-core as a test scope dep, it was excluded in the final artifact. Seems like a reactor dep bug. The short term work around is to remove the test scope from flume-ng-core.
