For this issue, I found some useful info in quiche's docs(https://docs.quic.tech/quiche/h3/index.html#http3-protocol-errors):

Once a http3 request is failed, the quiche will close the connection. But curl still keep the connection in pool and try reusing, that lead to a CONNECTION_CLOSE error and Operation timed out.
