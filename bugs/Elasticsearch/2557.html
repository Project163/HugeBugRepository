Discussion
- We realise Data Streams are handled differently in SLM/Snapshot/Restore compared to "normal" indices and there are different requirments/restrictions for their restoration into a running cluster (e.g. the Data Stream can't be restored if there's another Data Stream already present with the same name, etc.).

However, we'd expect to be able to exclude the Data Stream from our Snapshot and Restore just the "normal" indices without issue.

I'd guess this NPE is the result of the code not looking for Data Stream details when trying to gather information about the indices within the Snapshot (or something along those lines, from a very quick look at the stacktrace above).

The likely workaround (for us at least) will be to separate out Data Stream into their own SLM Policies, rather than keeping everything together in one as we have previously.
