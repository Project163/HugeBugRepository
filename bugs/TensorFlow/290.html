The fundamental issue here is that by changing the hidden size of the model we introduce a `TemporaryVariableOp` into the model which in turn causes XLA to fail because XLA doesn't support this op. Having the same fundamental network and just changing the hidden size of a model shouldn't introduce different types of operations into the network, I would expect this `TemporaryVariableOp` to be avoided at all costs to get the most XLA functionality.

This model is an incomplete `Transformer` model from the paper `Attention Is All You Need`, which is by no means a niche model.
