Title

LogService lock contention under heavy load

Description
Under heavy load, the LogService has severe lock contention in its LogListenerThread due to too coarse locks on m_entriesToDeliver and m_listeners. Also due to the 'single delivery' strategy, the performance of this service isn't that well under heavy load, it would be better to flush all available entries to all listeners in a single shot...
Discussion
- Attached a patch that will fix the lock contention and improves the performance by flushing multiple entries to all interested listeners.
