Discussion
- It isn't race.  tcp-disco-srvr  and  tcp-disco-msg-worker  are interrupted earlier than segmentation policy handles segmentation. See  org.apache.ignite.internal.managers.discovery.GridDiscoveryManager.DiscoveryWorker#onSegmentation  where we first disconnect SPI and then handle segmentation. 

 It seems could be fixed by adding check on SPI state in exception handler of  tcp-disco-srvr  and  tcp-disco-msg-worker .
