Title

ChangeSetPerformer not reliable for ZipArchiveInputStreams

Description
ChangeSetPerformer's perform function takes an ArchiveInputStream as an argument and thus frequently runs into issues described under heading 'ZipArchiveInputStream vs ZipFile' at  [URL]
