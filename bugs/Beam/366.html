Title

AvroIOTest is flaky

Description

 Steps to reproduce: 

 cd <path to beam>/runners/direct-java 
mvn clean compile 
mvn surefire:test@validates-runner-tests -Dtest=AvroIOTest 

 Repeat last step until a failure (on my machine failure rate is approx 1/3).
