Title

SQL "range(N) as alias" or "range(N) alias" doesn't work

Description
`select * from range(10) as A;` or `select * from range(10) A;` 
does not work. 
As a workaround, a subquery has to be used: 
`select * from (select * from range(10)) as A;`
