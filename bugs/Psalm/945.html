Title

Unexpected PossiblyFalseReference with elseif chain, but not with else if

Description
[URL]

Here, after `if .. elseif .. elseif` both vars are guaranteed to be not false. However Psalm marks one of them (depending on the order of `elseif`s) as possibly false.

Replacing `elseif`s with `else if` (they are equivalent) fixes the issue, which is also rather surprising.

- Why do you think it's redundant? On function enter there are 4 possible cases:

* `$from` and `$to` are both `null`
* `$from` is `DateTime` and `$to` is null
* `$from` is `null` and `$to` is `DateTime`
* `$from` and `$to` are both `DateTime`

`if .. elseif .. elseif` inspect and correct cases 1 through 3. Eliminating cases 1-2 does not leave case 3 as the only possible. The cases are independent, and the only reason they are not handled in separate `if`s is because vars are also changed (corrected) inside.
