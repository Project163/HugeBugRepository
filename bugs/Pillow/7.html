Title

Loading RGB image with tRNS header ignores transparency

Description
The PNG loader correctly loads palette images with tRNS value, but ignores the tRNS header with RGB images.
Discussion
- In looking into this a bit further, it looks like the transparency info is loaded correctly, but isn't taken into account when the image is saved or converted to RGBA.
- Yep. It appears that the convert routine takes the transparency value into account when there's a L or P image, but not when it's an RGB. It doesn't appear that there's a conversion anywhere in there that will take an individual value and turn it into an alpha mask.  

I've got something worked up that's about 90% of the way there.
