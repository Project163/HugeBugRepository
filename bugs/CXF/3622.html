 Just FYI, per the document here 
 [URL]

 JAX-WS client proxies are not thread safe, you should use synchronize access or use a pool of instances for the multiple client proxies you used in your code(which means you can't use a bunch of client proxies at the same time, instead you should synchronize access the client proxies). 

 CXF Client proxies are not thread safe for several cases, not only the conduit configuration(which is your case since the policy got involved here), but also a few other cases as mentioned in CXF  FAQ .
