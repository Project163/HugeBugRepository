- I also see that the job is being stuck because one of the tasks transitions from RUNNING to CANCELLED (instead of FINISHED).  
 This happens because RemoteInputChannel is polled after it was released. In such a case RemoteInputChannel  throws  CancelTaskException which triggers task cancellation. Cancellation prevents EndOfPartition event from being propagated and therefore downstream tasks keep running. 

   

 I've published a PR to prevent enqueing "released" channel (on receive); and to validate that the channel hasn't receive EoP (on poll).
