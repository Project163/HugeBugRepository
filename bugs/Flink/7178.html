- This issue arises because createDeploymentHandles requires a correspondence between executionsToDeploy and executionSlotAssignments ( here ), but after   FLINK-31843  , SimpleExecutionSlotAllocator broke this assumption ( here ). The error only occurs during schedule slow tasks by SpeculativeScheduler because AdaptiveBatchScheduler will schedule task one-by-one, while SpeculativeScheduler may schedule multiple slow tasks at once. This issue renders speculative execution completely unusable, so I will raise its priority and prepare a PR to fix it.
