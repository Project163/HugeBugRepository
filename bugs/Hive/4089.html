DESCRIBE of fully qualified table fails when db and table name match and non-default database is in use

Description

Discussion
- The getAttemptTableName() expects the fully qualified name to be just "table" or "table.column" or "table column". But it could also be of format "dbname.table" or "dbname.table column".  
The fix proposed factors in the fact that the DB name could be part of this qualified name.

 In the above describe statement, lintString (is an array of struct) is the columnName of the table and "myint" is the first field in the struct (mystring and underscore_int being other 2 fields).
