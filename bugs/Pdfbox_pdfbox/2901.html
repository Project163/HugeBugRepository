Title

Grayscale JPEG rendered multicolor

Description
Probably a PDFBox bug in handling a grayscale JPEG with a separation colorspace. The colorspace is here: 
  
 
Root/Pages/Kids/[0]/Resources/XObject/Fm0/Resources/XObject/Im0/ColorSpace
Discussion
- It is somewhat similar to   PDFBOX-3622  . Looking at the colorspace works fine, it shows different levels of a kind of blue. This means toRGB() works but toRGBImage() doesn't. I get a proper rendering by activating the   PDFBOX-3622   workaround in toRGBImage(). The similarity is that our separation colorspace is an ICC colorspace, but like   PDFBOX-3622   it is LAB-based.
