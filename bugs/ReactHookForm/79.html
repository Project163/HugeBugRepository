Title

useFieldArray() inputs cause re-rendering of whole form tree

Description
**Describe the bug**
Starting from version `4.9.4`, typing in field rendered by `useFieldArray()` triggers re-rendering of whole form tree.
Version `4.9.3` works fine

**To Reproduce**
Steps to reproduce the behavior:
1. Type anything in nested field input
2. Whole form tree is re-rendered

[URL]

**Expected behavior**
When typing in field, renders do not occur
