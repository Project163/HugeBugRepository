- To thread safe, the HashMap blockIdToSeqNumRanges "extends" SynchronizedMap (with mutable.SynchronizedMap). 

 In the onStart() function, we clear the blockIdToSeqNumRanges map. There, the clear() method is the one from SynchronizedMap: it's where the ClassCastException happens.
