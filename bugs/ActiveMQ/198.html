Discussion
- I patched this against the 4.0.1 version.  AMQ was ignoring any multicast URI other than the "multicast://default" one.
- This also appears to affect 4.1.0.
- patch applied with thanks!
- Actually I just rolled back the patch as it was breaking some things; folks are used to using the URI like this... 

  
 mutlicast: //group-name 
 
  

 if you want to override the discoveryURI then use 

  
 mutlicast: //group-name?discoveryURI=someURI
