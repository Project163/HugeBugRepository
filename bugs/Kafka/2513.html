Title

Client encountering SASL_HANDSHAKE protocol version errors on 2.5 / trunk

Description
I am trying 2.5.0 with sasl turned on and my consumer and producer clients receive: 
  
 org.apache.kafka.common.errors.UnsupportedVersionException: The SASL_HANDSHAKE protocol does not support version 2
 
  
 I believe this is due to  [URL] which added flexible version support and bumped the protocol version. 

 It appears that the SaslClientAuthenticator uses the max version for SASL_HANDSHAKE received in the broker's AP_VERSIONS response, and then uses that version even though it may not support it. See  [URL]  

 This may make it hard to ever evolve this schema. In the short term I suggest we roll back the version bump and flexible schema until we figure out a path forward. 

 It appears that this may not have been a problem in the past because the schema versions were the same and maybe we didn't validate the version number  [URL]
