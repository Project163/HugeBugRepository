Title

logback-core-1.2.12.jar vulnerability: CVE-2023-6481

Description
[URL]

 
 A serialization vulnerability in logback receiver component part of logback version 1.4.13, 1.3.13 and 1.2.12 allows an attacker to mount a Denial-Of-Service attack by sending poisoned data.
Discussion
- From the release notes: 

 
 Fixed vulnerability of a potential denial of service attack on a centralized logback receiver when a third party controlling a remote appender connects to said receiver and could shut down or slow down logging of events.  

 We don't offer logback in a remote configuration so I suggest we suppress.
